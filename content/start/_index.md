## 4. Software Requirements by Course

| Course | Required Software | Work Location | Cost |
|---|---|---|:---:|
| **Intro to Media Careers (IMC)** | Google Sites, Google Slides, Photopea, phone camera | Anywhere (browser & mobile) | Free |
