Welcome to Game Art & Design 1. In Semester 1, you will master 3D modeling and texturing in Blender, culminating in your Midterm Portfolio Prop. In Semester 2, you will transition into Unreal Engine 5, importing your assets, scripting mechanics with Blueprints, and building a fully playable Themed Parkour Platformer game.

**Course Scope:** 6 Units · 38 Modules & Assignments · 63 Standards Covered

---
