Welcome to Intro to Media Careers. In this course, you will build your foundational digital toolkit: graphic design, visual storytelling, digital ethics, and creative career planning. You will create projects using Google Sites, Canva, Photopea, mobile video, and presentation software, publishing everything directly to your personal online portfolio.

**Course Scope:** 6 Units · 43 Modules & Assignments · 100 Standards Covered

---

## Unit 1: Digital Foundations & Portfolio Launch

> **What you will learn:** In this unit, you will build and launch your professional Google Sites portfolio website, which will house all of your creative work this year. You will master file management and naming conventions, evaluate computer hardware specifications, secure your accounts, and establish baseline typing speed.
