## How Your Learning Is Structured

Your assignments and readings are organized to help you learn by doing:

- **Action First**: You learn best by building real projects. Each assignment pairs with one or two short preparation modules so you get the key concepts quickly and spend your lab time creating.
- **Bite-Sized Modules**: Lessons are focused (4 to 6 minutes of video or quick reading) with self-check questions to verify your understanding before you begin your project.
- **Reference Library**: In addition to required readings, each unit includes a reference shelf with extra guides and tutorials you can explore whenever you want to level up your skills.
