## Student Questions & Portfolio Submissions

{{< details title="How do I submit my completed lab assignments?" closed="true" >}}
Every assignment spec includes step-by-step submission instructions:
1. Export your final project render, screenshot, or video artifact.
2. Upload the artifact to your **Google Sites Student Portfolio** under the corresponding unit page.
3. Include a short description detailing your process, tools used, and any challenges resolved.
4. Copy your published Google Sites page URL and turn it in on **Google Classroom**.
{{< /details >}}

{{< details title="What software do I need to install for these courses?" closed="true" >}}
All core software used in the AED Module Library is free or open-source:
- **3D Modeling & Animation**: [Blender](https://www.blender.org/) (Free & Open Source)
- **Game Engine Development**: [Unreal Engine 5](https://www.unrealengine.com/) (Free for Education & Developers)
- **2D Image Editing & Texturing**: [Krita](https://krita.org/) or GIMP (Free & Open Source)
- **Reference Management**: [PureRef](https://www.pureref.com/) (Free / Pay-What-You-Want)
{{< /details >}}

{{< details title="What if my home computer cannot run Unreal Engine 5?" closed="true" >}}
All lab specs are designed with flexible hardware fallbacks:
- If your home PC cannot run Unreal Engine 5, you can complete 3D modeling and UV unwrapping tasks in Blender, or focus on pre-production planning (PureRef mood boards, storyboards, and mini GDDs).
- Classroom lab workstations are equipped to run all Unreal Engine 5 assignments during class studio hours.
{{< /details >}}

{{< details title="How are my assignments evaluated and graded?" closed="true" >}}
Every assignment spec includes a standardized 3-part rubric:
- **Technical Execution (4-5 pts)**: Artifact meets all technical specifications and industry practices.
- **Documentation & Process (4-5 pts)**: Thorough process notes, references, and breakdown included.
- **Portfolio Presentation (4-5 pts)**: Well-organized on Google Sites with clear titles and summaries.
{{< /details >}}

## Teacher & Curriculum Guidance

{{< details title="How do I sequence these modules for a 1-year or 3-year CTE pathway?" closed="true" >}}
Visit the [**Course Maps**](/courses/) section to view complete unit pathways:
- [**Intro to Media Careers (IMC)**](/courses/imc/): 1-year introductory foundation pathway.
- [**Game Art & Design 1 (GAD1)**](/courses/gad1/): Year 1 of the Game Art & Design pathway (3D modeling, texturing, Unreal logic).
- [**Game Art & Design 2 (GAD2)**](/courses/gad2/): Year 2 capstone pathway (technical art, rigging, team vertical slice).
{{< /details >}}

{{< details title="How do these modules align with California CTE Model Curriculum Standards?" closed="true" >}}
Every module and assignment is tagged with specific California AME Model Curriculum Standards. Visit the [**Standards Index**](/standards/) to explore Anchor Standards (1.0–12.0), Interdisciplinary Standards (13.0–16.0), and Pathway Standards.
{{< /details >}}
