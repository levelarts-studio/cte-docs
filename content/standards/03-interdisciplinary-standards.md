## **Arts, Media, and Entertainment: Interdisciplinary Standards** 

The 2023 AME Interdisciplinary Standards are relevant to students in all AME Pathways. These standards outline the importance of business and marketing, legal responsibility, media content creation and project management skills across creative industries. These standards build off the Anchor Standards and should be integrated into all pathway instruction across AME Pathways and Focus Areas.

### **13.0-16.0 AME Interdisciplinary Standards** 

#### **13.0 Business and Marketing**

*Understand and employ effective business and marketing strategies.*

- **13.1** &mdash; Develop and present stories that demonstrate the value of a product, service, or experience.

- **13.2** &mdash; Pitch an original concept with a visual presentation and engaging narrative.

- **13.3** &mdash; Develop and/or maintain a visually compelling and appropriate set of branded elements for a product, service, or experience.

- **13.4** &mdash; Explore content monetization using different social media, distribution, and streaming platforms.

- **13.5** &mdash; Create and/or adhere to a project and/or production budget.

- **13.6** &mdash; Interact professionally with clients and help define clear and actionable objectives that satisfy their goals.

- **13.7** &mdash; Develop and apply business acumen skills to advance creative and/or entrepreneurial ideas.

#### **14.0 Legal Responsibility**

*Understand contracts, laws, regulations, and organizational norms.*

- **14.1** &mdash; Examine how copyright laws impact content creation, derivative works, performance, and publishing across various platforms.

- **14.2** &mdash; Adhere to terms of use, including copyright laws and regulations.

- **14.3** &mdash; Appropriately cite proprietary information.

- **14.4** &mdash; Use correct terminology as it applies to contracts and legal.

- **14.5** &mdash; Read and interpret a contract and/or legal document.

- **14.6** &mdash; Apply rules and regulations regarding sharing of confidential information as determined by workplace practices including Non-Disclosure Agreements (NDA).

- **14.7** &mdash; Examine process for registering original creative works to protect intellectual property (IP).

- **14.8** &mdash; Understand steps and documents required to start a non-profit or for-profit company.

#### **15.0 Media Content Creation**

*Develop and create original content.*

- **15.1** &mdash; Tell engaging and compelling stories using a variety of artistic mediums and/or media formats.

- **15.2** &mdash; Develop original content for a specific media platform based on format, length, and audience.

- **15.3** &mdash; Clearly communicate a story using different industry-specific methods (i.e., outline, script, rundown, treatment, and synopsis.) prior to capturing or creating content.

- **15.4** &mdash; Use storyboarding and/or shot lists to plan an engaging and clear scene or sequence.

- **15.5** &mdash; Use angles, composition, framing, shots, and editing to enhance visual storytelling.

- **15.6** &mdash; Use music and audio to enhance storytelling.

- **15.7** &mdash; Generate and integrate audio elements (i.e., sound effects, dialog, and music) into seamless and effective sequences.

- **15.8** &mdash; Review content to identify and resolve problems, ensure seamless transitions, and maximize the audience’s experience.

#### **16.0 Production and Project Management**

*Understand production pipeline and project workflow.*

- **16.1** &mdash; Identify and differentiate tools and equipment (including name, function, and purpose) to demonstrate proficiency required for safe and proper use and operation.

- **16.2** &mdash; Understand roles and responsibilities for each member of a production or project team and related communications etiquette.

- **16.3** &mdash; Properly handle, operate, maintain, document, and store supplies, tools, equipment, scenic and costume items, cables, and/or accessories.

- **16.4** &mdash; Analyze and breakdown creative plans[^5] to identify specific production and project needs and logistics (i.e., location/venue, casting, insurance, permits, scheduling, budgeting, and crewing.)

- **16.5** &mdash; Attend production/project meetings to discuss creative plans, timelines, and division of duties.

- **16.6** &mdash; Interact professionally with technical crew, talent, developers, and/or staff to realize creative ideas and execute project needs.

- **16.7** &mdash; Track tasks and deliverables using industry-standard project management strategies and software.
