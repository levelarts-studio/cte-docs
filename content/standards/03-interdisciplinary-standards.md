The **Cross-Pathway Standards (13.0–16.0)** are interdisciplinary and address competencies that apply across all creative industries: business and marketing, legal compliance and IP, media content creation, and collaborative production pipelines.

{{< callout type="info" >}}
**100% Pathway Coverage**: All 36 Cross-Pathway Standards (13.0–16.0) are embedded throughout student projects, pitch decks, client briefs, and studio production milestones.
{{< /callout >}}

## 13.0 Business, Marketing, & Public Relations

- **13.1 Storytelling for Product Value and Impact** &mdash; Develop and present stories that demonstrate the value of a product, service, or experience. &nbsp;[View Aligned Modules &rarr;](/standards/13.1/)

- **13.2 Pitching Creative Concepts** &mdash; Pitch an original concept with a visual presentation and narrative that clearly communicates the idea and engages the audience. &nbsp;[View Aligned Modules &rarr;](/standards/13.2/)

- **13.3 Brand Identity & Visual Merchandising** &mdash; Develop, maintain, and present a visually compelling and consistent brand identity across products, services, experiences; design merchandising displays to reinforce brand recognition and establish visual impact. &nbsp;[View Aligned Modules &rarr;](/standards/13.3/)

- **13.4 Content Monetization Strategies** &mdash; Identify and compare ways creators earn income by analyzing examples of monetization strategies used on social media, video sharing, and streaming platforms. &nbsp;[View Aligned Modules &rarr;](/standards/13.4/)

- **13.5 Managing Budgets** &mdash; Develop and manage budgets for creative projects by forecasting costs, allocating resources, tracking expenditures, and evaluating return on investment (ROI). &nbsp;[View Aligned Modules &rarr;](/standards/13.5/)

- **13.6 Client Interaction** &mdash; Listen to client or customer needs, interact professionally, and help define clear and actionable objectives that satisfy their goals. &nbsp;[View Aligned Modules &rarr;](/standards/13.6/)

- **13.7 Business Acumen** &mdash; Develop business acumen to advance a creative idea in a business life cycle (i.e., collaborating with key interest holders, networking to identify partnerships, pitching and refining business concepts, maintaining a brand identity, managing business finances, and sourcing and maintaining client relationships). &nbsp;[View Aligned Modules &rarr;](/standards/13.7/)

- **13.8 Public Relations (PR)** &mdash; Apply PR strategies including media and audience engagement to the development of campaigns, press kits, crisis management, and publicity planning. &nbsp;[View Aligned Modules &rarr;](/standards/13.8/)

- **13.9 Understanding the Content Ecosystem** &mdash; Identify the relationships between content creators, publishers, developers, distributors, marketers, and retailers, and package content to reach an intended audience. &nbsp;[View Aligned Modules &rarr;](/standards/13.9/)

## 14.0 Legal Responsibility

- **14.1 Intellectual Property (IP)Types** &mdash; Identify the different types of intellectual property and related laws (e.g., copyright, trademark, rights of publicity, and patents) and explain how they protect creative and commercial assets. &nbsp;[View Aligned Modules &rarr;](/standards/14.1/)

- **14.2 Fair Use** &mdash; Define the factors used to determine fair use and analyze how emerging technology (e.g., generative AI) challenges traditional concepts of authorship, ownership, and fair use. &nbsp;[View Aligned Modules &rarr;](/standards/14.2/)

- **14.3 Protecting Confidentiality** &mdash; Apply rules and regulations regarding sharing of confidential information as determined by workplace practices including Non-Disclosure Agreements (NDAs). &nbsp;[View Aligned Modules &rarr;](/standards/14.3/)

- **14.4 Contracts & Contract Terminology** &mdash; Analyze contracts and/or legal documents to identify fair terms and ensure protection of creative IP and ownership rights; use correct terminology as it applies to contracts and legal matters. &nbsp;[View Aligned Modules &rarr;](/standards/14.4/)

- **14.5 Terms of Use** &mdash; Comply with the rules, regulations and conditions set by a platform, service, or website when accessing or using its resources. &nbsp;[View Aligned Modules &rarr;](/standards/14.5/)

- **14.6 Fraud Prevention** &mdash; Analyze strategies for fraud prevention by recognizing common threats, protecting sensitive information, and following privacy and compliance regulations in business and marketing settings. &nbsp;[View Aligned Modules &rarr;](/standards/14.6/)

- **14.7 Employment Law** &mdash; Examine employment laws related to hiring, wages, workplace safety, labor unions, and discrimination. &nbsp;[View Aligned Modules &rarr;](/standards/14.7/)

- **14.8 Freelance Rights** &mdash; Identify worker classifications, contract terms, compensation rights, and ethical responsibilities for independent contractors (freelance) and full-time creative roles. &nbsp;[View Aligned Modules &rarr;](/standards/14.8/)

- **14.9 Comparing Business Entity Types** &mdash; Differentiate types of businesses (e.g., sole proprietorship, partnership, limited liability corporation, S corporation, public benefit corporation, and 501(c) 3 organization). &nbsp;[View Aligned Modules &rarr;](/standards/14.9/)

## 15.0 Media Content Creation

- **15.1 Narrative Storytelling** &mdash; Evaluate and demonstrate how narrative structure, point of view, character, conflict, theme, and setting impact story development. &nbsp;[View Aligned Modules &rarr;](/standards/15.1/)

- **15.2 Platform-Specific Content** &mdash; Develop original content for a specific media platform based on format, length, and intended audience. &nbsp;[View Aligned Modules &rarr;](/standards/15.2/)

- **15.3 Authentic Representation** &mdash; Create content with authentic representation of diverse people, communities, perspectives, and stories. &nbsp;[View Aligned Modules &rarr;](/standards/15.3/)

- **15.5 Pre-Production Planning** &mdash; Develop and communicate a story using different industry-specific methods (e.g., outline, script, rundown, treatment, storyboarding, and shot lists) prior to capturing or creating content. &nbsp;[View Aligned Modules &rarr;](/standards/15.5/)

- **15.6 Cinematic Techniques** &mdash; Use angles, composition, framing, shots, and editing to enhance visual storytelling. &nbsp;[View Aligned Modules &rarr;](/standards/15.6/)

- **15.8 Editing Fundamentals** &mdash; Assemble and refine audio or visual content, ensuring clarity, continuity, and audience engagement; use industry-standard editing techniques appropriate to the project. &nbsp;[View Aligned Modules &rarr;](/standards/15.8/)

- **15.9 Rendering & Exporting** &mdash; Apply rendering and exporting techniques to comply with industry standards. &nbsp;[View Aligned Modules &rarr;](/standards/15.9/)

## 16.0 Production & Project Management

- **16.1 Production Tools & Equipment** &mdash; Identify supplies, tools, and equipment by name, function, and purpose; properly handle, operate, maintain, and document supplies, tools, and equipment used in exhibition, interior design, and production environments. &nbsp;[View Aligned Modules &rarr;](/standards/16.1/)

- **16.2 Roles & Responsibilities** &mdash; Evaluate roles and responsibilities for each member of a production or project team; work collaboratively and interact professionally to meet directorial goals, realize creative ideas, and execute project needs. &nbsp;[View Aligned Modules &rarr;](/standards/16.2/)

- **16.3 Event & Production Planning** &mdash; Design, plan and execute productions, activations, and events for different venue types, sites, locations, audiences, and purposes. &nbsp;[View Aligned Modules &rarr;](/standards/16.3/)

- **16.4 Creative Planning** &mdash; Develop and analyze creative plans to identify project or production needs (e.g., creative brief, script, storyboard, game design document, setlist, and schematic). &nbsp;[View Aligned Modules &rarr;](/standards/16.4/)

- **16.5 Budgeting & Scheduling** &mdash; Develop, maintain, and execute a project or production budget and explain the importance and interrelationship between timelines and budget constraints. &nbsp;[View Aligned Modules &rarr;](/standards/16.5/)

- **16.6 Production Phases & Workflow** &mdash; Differentiate components and workflow for each stage associated with the preproduction, production, and post-production of a creative project. &nbsp;[View Aligned Modules &rarr;](/standards/16.6/)

- **16.7 Collaboration Software** &mdash; Contribute to multi-phase projects by managing shared tasks through professional project management software technology, collaboration platforms, shared drives, and version-controlled files. &nbsp;[View Aligned Modules &rarr;](/standards/16.7/)

- **16.8 Project & Budget Meetings** &mdash; Facilitate and contribute to production and project management meetings to discuss creative plans, budgets, timelines, constraints, responsibilities, and division of duties. &nbsp;[View Aligned Modules &rarr;](/standards/16.8/)

- **16.9 Logistics & Resource Planning** &mdash; Identify specific production or project needs and logistics (e.g., timeline, location/venue, casting, funding, insurance, permits, equipment, blueprints or schematics, scheduling, and crewing/staffing); adhere to all workplace safety standards and protocols for performance venues or locations. &nbsp;[View Aligned Modules &rarr;](/standards/16.9/)
