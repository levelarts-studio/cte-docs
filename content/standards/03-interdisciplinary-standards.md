The **Interdisciplinary Standards (13.0–16.0)** outline business, legal responsibility, media creation, and project management skills relevant to all AME Pathways.

{{< callout type="info" >}}
**Cross-Curricular Integration**: Interdisciplinary standards build upon the Anchor Standards and should be integrated across all pathway instruction and student portfolio deliverables.
{{< /callout >}}

---

## 13.0 Business and Marketing

*Understand and employ effective business and marketing strategies.*

- **13.1** &mdash; Develop and present stories that demonstrate the value of a product, service, or experience.
- **13.2** &mdash; Pitch an original concept with a visual presentation and engaging narrative.
- **13.3** &mdash; Develop and/or maintain a visually compelling set of branded elements for a product, service, or experience.
- **13.4** &mdash; Explore content monetization using social media, distribution, and streaming platforms.
- **13.5** &mdash; Create and/or adhere to a project and/or production budget.
- **13.6** &mdash; Interact professionally with clients and help define clear and actionable objectives that satisfy their goals.
- **13.7** &mdash; Develop and apply business acumen skills to advance creative and entrepreneurial ideas.

---

## 14.0 Legal Responsibility

*Understand contracts, laws, regulations, and organizational norms.*

- **14.1** &mdash; Examine how copyright laws impact content creation, derivative works, performance, and publishing.
- **14.2** &mdash; Adhere to terms of use, including copyright laws and regulations.
- **14.3** &mdash; Appropriately cite proprietary information.
- **14.4** &mdash; Use correct terminology as it applies to contracts and legal documents.
- **14.5** &mdash; Read and interpret a contract and legal document.
- **14.6** &mdash; Apply rules regarding sharing of confidential information as determined by Non-Disclosure Agreements (NDA).
- **14.7** &mdash; Examine the process for registering original creative works to protect intellectual property (IP).
- **14.8** &mdash; Understand steps and documents required to start a non-profit or for-profit company.

---

## 15.0 Media Content Creation

*Develop and create original content.*

- **15.1** &mdash; Tell engaging and compelling stories using a variety of artistic mediums and media formats.
- **15.2** &mdash; Develop original content for a specific media platform based on format, length, and audience.
- **15.3** &mdash; Clearly communicate a story using outlines, scripts, rundowns, treatments, and synopses.
- **15.4** &mdash; Use storyboarding and shot lists to plan an engaging and clear scene or sequence.
- **15.5** &mdash; Use camera angles, composition, framing, shots, and editing to enhance visual storytelling.
- **15.6** &mdash; Use music and audio to enhance storytelling.
- **15.7** &mdash; Generate and integrate audio elements (sound effects, dialog, music) into seamless sequences.
- **15.8** &mdash; Review content to identify and resolve problems, ensure seamless transitions, and maximize audience experience.

---

## 16.0 Production and Project Management

*Understand production pipeline and project workflow.*

- **16.1** &mdash; Identify and differentiate tools and equipment for safe and proper operation.
- **16.2** &mdash; Understand roles and responsibilities for each member of a production team.
- **16.3** &mdash; Properly handle, operate, maintain, document, and store supplies and equipment.
- **16.4** &mdash; Analyze and breakdown creative plans to identify specific production needs and logistics.
- **16.5** &mdash; Attend production/project meetings to discuss creative plans, timelines, and division of duties.
- **16.6** &mdash; Interact professionally with technical crew, talent, developers, and staff to execute project needs.
- **16.7** &mdash; Track tasks and deliverables using industry-standard project management strategies and tools.
