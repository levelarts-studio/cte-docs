The **Career Ready Standards (1.0–12.0)** outline the essential skills, knowledge, and behaviors students need to succeed in any workplace or creative industry. Flexible by design, these standards emphasize transferable skills that prepare students for employment, postsecondary education, and long-term career growth.

{{< callout type="info" >}}
**100% Pathway Coverage**: All 99 Career Ready Standards (1.0–12.0) are actively taught and evaluated across our three-year pathway through studio work habits, lab practices, and technical assignments.
{{< /callout >}}
