## How Lab Assignments Work

Every assignment spec in the library follows a predictable, transparent structure designed to support independent studio work in lab:

1. **Prerequisite Reading First**: Always check the **Before You Start** section of the spec. Reading the 5-to-10 minute module ensures you know the shortcuts and core techniques before opening software.
2. **Build the Artifact**: Work step-by-step through the lab instructions. Use screenshots, work-in-progress exports, and reference boards to document your iterations.
3. **Publish to Google Sites**: Every spec defines exact portfolio requirements (image resolutions, video formats, embed codes, and artist reflection prompts).
4. **Submit on Google Classroom**: Copy your live published Google Sites portfolio URL and submit it to the matching assignment slot in Google Classroom.
