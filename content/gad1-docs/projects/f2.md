You will create a **playable environment showcase** in Unreal Engine that integrates your **rigged character, props, and environment**. This project connects directly to **Year 2, Semester 2** units:  
- **Unit 4 — Unreal Engine Basics**  
- **Unit 5 — Environment Design in Unreal**  
- **Unit 6 — Blueprint Scripting Basics**  

The focus is on building a **small but polished interactive scene**. You are not expected to make a full game — your goal is to demonstrate control over importing assets, designing environments, and scripting simple interactivity.

> [!NOTE]  
> In **Year 3**, you will expand these skills to create a **team-based vertical slice**. This project is your first step toward industry-style production in Unreal.

---

# Steps to Completion

{{% steps %}}

### Step 1 — Environment Blockout  
Create a simple greybox level in Unreal.  
Focus on **scale and proportion** so your character fits correctly in the world.  

### Step 2 — Set Dressing & Lighting  
Add props, textures, and materials.  
Use basic lights (directional, skylight, spot/point) and experiment with atmosphere.  

### Step 3 — Character Integration  
Import your **rigged character** and confirm materials, scale, and placement.  
Pose or idle animation optional for polish.  

### Step 4 — Blueprint Interactivity  
Add at least one **trigger or pickup**.  
Implement basic **character movement** and a **simple win condition** (goal reached, item collected, etc.).  

### Step 5 — Renders & Playthrough  
Capture **screenshots** of the environment with your character.  
Record a short (30–60 sec) **playthrough video** showing interactivity.  

### Step 6 — Final Showcase Package  
Compile your environment, character, and Blueprints into:  
- Unreal packaged build + project folder  
- Presentation board with renders and notes  
- Process PDF with Blueprint screenshots, references, and reflection  

{{% /steps %}}

## Rubric (100 pts)

| Criteria | Excellent | Adequate | Weak | Value |
|----------|-----------|----------|------|------:|
| **Environment** | Clear scale, polished lighting, cohesive composition | Basic scene built, some issues | Incomplete or sloppy | 25 |
| **Character Integration** | Fully imported, materials/textures correct | Imported, minor issues | Missing or broken | 15 |
| **Blueprints** | Movement, trigger, and win condition work smoothly | Basic movement or one interactive element | No working interactivity | 25 |
| **Renders & Playthrough** | Polished visuals, clean video capture | Present but rough | Poor readability | 15 |
| **Process & PDF** | Detailed workflow, Blueprint screenshots, reflection | Partial documentation | Minimal/no documentation | 10 |
| **Professional Practices** | Correct naming, folder structure, references credited | Minor errors | Disorganized | 10 |

*Late work: −10% per week. Missing references: −5 pts. Poor naming: −2 pts.*
