You will design **one original “hero prop”** that serves as the centerpiece of a larger game world. This project connects directly to **Year 2, Semester 1** units:  
- **Unit 1 — Blender Fundamentals & Basic Props**  
- **Unit 2 — Intermediate Prop Modeling & UV Mapping**  
- **Unit 3 — Texturing, Lighting & Export Pipeline**  

Your prop should show clear **storytelling through design** — how does its form, material, and wear hint at its origin and role in the world?  

> [!NOTE]  
> In **Semester 2**, you will expand this project into a full **environment in Unreal Engine** (Units 4–6). Plan ahead for how your prop shapes its world.

---

# Deliverables

Submit one complete **Hero Prop Package** that includes:

1. **Prop Narrative (150–200 words)**  
   - What the prop is, who uses it, and why it matters in the game world.  

2. **Concept Art**  
   - Mood board with references (credited).  
   - 3–5 sketches or silhouettes exploring form.  
   - Final orthographic sheet (front, side, top).  

3. **3D Model**  
   - Low-poly model in Blender (clean topology, correct scale).  
   - UV unwrap with non-overlap and consistent texel density.  

4. **Textures & Renders**  
   - Base color + roughness/metallic + normal/AO.  
   - One clay render and one beauty render with lighting + turntable.  

5. **Presentation Board + Process PDF**  
   - Single board showing final prop, callouts, and narrative.  
   - PDF including iteration stages, references with credit, and short reflection.  

> [!WARNING]  
> This is **not an art contest**. Grading values **iteration, storytelling, and workflow**. Use references and credit them.

## Learning Outcomes

By completing this project, you will:  
- Apply **game art workflows** from concept to game-ready asset.  
- Practice **modeling, UVs, and texturing** using Blender.  
- Document and present a prop as part of a **professional portfolio**.  
- Prepare an asset pipeline for **Unreal integration** in Semester 2.

## Submission Format

- Folder labeled: `Last_First_HeroProp.zip`  
- Subfolders:  
  - `01_Concept/` → sketches, orthographic, mood board  
  - `02_Models/` → `.blend` and `.fbx`  
  - `03_Textures/` → `.png` texture maps  
  - `04_Renders/` → beauty + clay renders + turntable  
  - `05_Documents/` → narrative + process PDF

## Rubric (100 pts)

| Criteria | Excellent | Adequate | Weak | Value |
|----------|-----------|----------|------|------:|
| **Narrative** | Clear story role, detailed context | Basic description | Vague or none | 10 |
| **Concept Art** | Iterative sketches, clean orthographic | Some sketches, final view | Minimal work | 20 |
| **Modeling** | Clean topology, scaled, readable | Minor issues | Sloppy or incomplete | 25 |
| **UVs & Textures** | Organized, seamless, logical materials | Some errors | Missing/incomplete | 20 |
| **Renders & Board** | Polished, well-lit, labeled | Present but rough | Poor readability | 15 |
| **Process & Professionalism** | Complete PDF, references credited | Some missing | No documentation | 10 |

*Late work: −10% per week. Missing references: −5 pts. Poor naming: −2 pts.*
