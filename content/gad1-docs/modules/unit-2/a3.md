## 🛠️ Workshop Goals

- Model your **Wall** (4m x 4m).
- Unwrap it and place UVs over the "Wall" part of your Atlas.
- Model your **Floor** (4m x 4m).
- Unwrap to "Floor" texture.
- **Test**: Duplicate them in Blender. Do they snap together perfectly?
