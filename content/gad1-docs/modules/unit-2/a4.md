## 🎥 Video Outline

- **Breaking the Repetition**
  - Modular kits look repetitive.
  - "Hero Assets" are unique, detailed props that draw the eye.
- **Storytelling**
  - A generic wall tells no story.
  - A "busted wall with graffiti" tells a story.

## 📚 Resources

## 🛠️ Workshop Goals

- Create **ONE** unique prop (Statue, Fountain, Throne, Destroyed Pillar).
- This does **NOT** need to be on the strict grid.
- This can have its own unique texture (doesn't need to be on the atlas).
- Focus on detail and silhouette.

## 📑 Deliverables

- **Render/Screenshot** of your Hero Asset.
- Upload to **Canvas**.
