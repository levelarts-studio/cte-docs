## 🏁 Final Unit Challenge

You are now a Level Designer. Take your modular kit and your hero asset, and build a room.

## ✅ Checklist

- [ ] **Assembly**: Build a hallway or room using your modular pieces.
- [ ] **Placement**: Place your Hero Asset in a focal point.
- [ ] **Lighting**: Add point lights (Torches? Lamps?) to create mood.
- [ ] **Camera**: Find a cool angle.
- [ ] **Portfolio**:
  - [ ] Create "Unit 2" page.
  - [ ] Upload final Render.
  - [ ] Upload the Texture Atlas.
  - [ ] Upload a screenshot of the "Kit" (pieces separated).

## 🚀 Submission Guidelines

1. **Render** your scene.
2. **Post** to ArtStation.
3. **Submit** link to Canvas.

## 💯 Grading Rubric

- [ ] **Modularity (40%)**: Do the pieces snap together seamlessly?
- [ ] **Lighting (30%)**: Is the scene mood set correctly?
- [ ] **Optimization (30%)**: Texture Atlas used correctly.
