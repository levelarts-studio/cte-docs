## 🎥 Video Outline

- **What is an Atlas?**
  - One image file, many textures.
  - Draws less power from the GPU (1 Draw call vs 10).
- **Creating the Atlas**
  - Finding seamless textures (Brick, Wood, Metal).
  - Combining them in Krita/Photoshop into a 2048x2048 grid.

## 📚 Resources

- **PolyHaven (Free Textures)** — [Link](#)

## 🛠️ Workshop Goals

- Find 4 textures: Wall, Floor, Trim, Metal.
- Create a **2048x2048** canvas in Krita.
- Place the textures into quadrants.
- Export as `Atlas_Albedo.png`.

## 📑 Deliverables

- **The PNG File** of your texture atlas.
- Upload to **Canvas**.
