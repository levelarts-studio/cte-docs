## 🏁 Final Unit Challenge

Your character is modeled, textured, rigged, and animated. It is alive.

## ✅ Checklist

- [ ] **Animations**: Idle Loop and Attack Action.
- [ ] **Bake**: Animations are "baked" to the timeline.
- [ ] **Export**: FBX file contains Mesh + Rig + Animation.
- [ ] **Portfolio**:
  - [ ] Create "Unit 5" page.
  - [ ] Upload video of both animations.
  - [ ] Explain your rigging process (screenshots of bones).

## 🚀 Submission Guidelines

1. **Render** your animations side-by-side.
2. **Post** to ArtStation.
3. **Submit** link to Canvas.

## 💯 Grading Rubric

- [ ] **Rigging (40%)**: Clean deformations?
- [ ] **Animation (30%)**: Does the attack have weight?
- [ ] **Looping (30%)**: Is the idle seamless?
