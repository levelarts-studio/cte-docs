  Rigging & Animation (The Pipeline)
</div>

A beautiful model is useless if it can't move. In this unit, students learn the "Skeleton" of 3D art. They will build a digital armature, paint "Influence Weights" (Skinning), and create two game-ready animation loops.

{{< callout type="info" >}}
This unit is technically demanding. **Weight Painting** is often considered the most frustrating part of the pipeline. Patience is required.
{{< /callout >}}

## Topics Covered

- **Rigging**: Creating bones and hierarchies.
- **Inverse Kinematics (IK)**: Why foot controls are better than rotating every knee bone.
- **Weight Painting**: Teaching the mesh which bone to follow.
- **Keyframe Animation**: The 12 Principles (Squash, Stretch, Timing).
- **Video Game Loops**: Creating animations that loop perfectly.

## Learning Report

**Objectives**

- Create a Humanoid Armature (Skeleton).
- Bind a Mesh to the Skeleton (Parenting).
- Paint Skin Weights to fix deformation issues.
- Animate an "Idle" and "Attack" cycle.

**Materials**

- Blender.
- User's Character Model from Unit 4 (Retopologized w/ Normal Maps).

**Standards Alignment**

- **17.6** — Apply principles of rigging to a digital character
- **10.3** — Visualize ideas through sketches, turnarounds, and modeling
- **4.3** — Use job-specific hardware/software for workflows and content delivery
