  Environment Design & Level Assembly
</div>

This unit focuses on designing and assembling a playable environment inside Unreal Engine. Students will learn to block out spaces, place props, sculpt landscapes, and add foliage. The unit ends with lighting and post-processing to set the mood and atmosphere.

{{< callout type="info" >}}
This unit introduces **environment design principles** in a game engine. By the end, students will have a basic environment with props, landscape, foliage, and lighting — ready to expand into the final project.
{{< /callout >}}

## Topics Covered
- Blocking out environments with geometry tools  
- Using props to build composition and scale  
- Sculpting terrain with the landscape tool  
- Painting foliage and natural details  
- Setting up lighting and post-processing for mood

## Learning Report

**Objectives**  
- Block out functional spaces using Unreal’s geometry tools  
- Place props effectively to establish scale and storytelling  
- Sculpt landscapes and apply terrain materials  
- Paint foliage to add natural detail and believability  
- Apply lighting and post-processing to create atmosphere  

**Materials**  
- Classroom computer with Unreal Engine installed  
- Props created in Blender (Units 1–3)  
- Unreal starter content for landscapes, foliage, and lights  

**Assessment**  
Students demonstrate their understanding by building a small environment scene that integrates blockouts, props, landscape, foliage, and lighting. Evaluation focuses on **technical workflow** and **clear design choices**, not on complexity or polish.  

**Activities**  
Students will block out spaces, experiment with prop placement, sculpt terrain, paint foliage, and apply lighting. Each step will be practiced in class, with peer review of composition and mood.
