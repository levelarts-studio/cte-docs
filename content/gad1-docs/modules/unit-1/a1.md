## 🎥 Video Outline

- **The Viewport**
  - Orbit (Middle Mouse).
  - Pan (Shift + Middle Mouse).
  - Zoom (Scroll).
- **Object Mode vs Edit Mode**
  - **Tab**: The most important key in Blender.
  - **1, 2, 3**: Verts, Edges, Faces.
- **Transform Tools**
  - **G**: Grab (Move).
  - **R**: Rotate.
  - **S**: Scale.
