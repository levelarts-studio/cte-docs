  - Importing Front and Side views.

  - Extrude shoulders -> Arms.
  - Extrude Hips -> Legs.
- **Proportions**
  - "The wrists fall at the crotch line."

## 🛠️ Workshop Goals

- Import your references.
- Create a low-poly blockout of the full body (minus hands/feet/head).
- Focus on **Silhoutte** and **Joint Placement** (Make sure knees/elbows bend in the right place!).
