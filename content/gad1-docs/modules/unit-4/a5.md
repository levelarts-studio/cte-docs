## 🏁 Final Unit Challenge

You have a game-ready character with high-fidelity details baked into normal maps. It should look like a PS4-era character model.

## ✅ Checklist

- [ ] **Poly Count**: Under 15,000 Tris.
- [ ] **Textures**: Albedo, Normal, Roughness maps applied.
- [ ] **Lighting**: 3-Point Light setup.
- [ ] **Portfolio**:
  - [ ] Create "Unit 4" page.
  - [ ] Upload Turntable Video.
  - [ ] Upload "Texture Breakdown" (Show the separate maps).

## 🚀 Submission Guidelines

1. **Render** your final turntable.
2. **Post** to ArtStation.
3. **Submit** link to Canvas.

## 💯 Grading Rubric

- [ ] **Bake Quality (40%)**: No artifacts or weird shadows.
- [ ] **Detail (30%)**: Does the normal map pop?
- [ ] **Optimization (30%)**: Clean topology under the hood.
