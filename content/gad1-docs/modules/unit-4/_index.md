<div class="hx:mt-1 hx:text-gray-500 hx:text-lg">
  Character Modeling II (Sculpting & Bake)
</div>

This unit takes the "Greybox" character from Unit 3 and turns it into a Triple-A game asset. Students learn digital sculpting to add organic detail, then learn the technical art wizardry of "Baking" to project that detail onto a game-ready mesh.

{{< callout type="info" >}}
This unit bridges the gap between **Digital Art** (Sculpting) and **Technical Art** (Retopology and Baking).
{{< /callout >}}

## Topics Covered

- **Digital Sculpting**: Brushes, Dynatopo, and Multires.
- **High Poly Workflow**: Adding millions of polygons for detail.
- **Retopology**: Drawing a low-poly shell over the high-poly sculpt.
- **Normal Maps**: The magic of fake detail.
- **Texture Baking**: Projecting detail from High to Low.

## Learning Report

**Objectives**

- Use digital sculpting tools to create organic details (muscles, cloth folds).
- Understand the difference between High Poly (Art) and Low Poly (Game).
- Create a clean "Retopology" mesh.
- Bake High Poly normals onto the Low Poly mesh.

**Materials**

- Blender (Sculpt Mode).
- Drawing Tablet (Highly Recommended).

**Standards Alignment**

- **18.2** — Understand artist workflows for modeling, retopology, and UVs
- **4.5** — Apply rendering and exporting techniques to meet standards
- **4.3** — Use job-specific hardware/software for workflows and delivery
