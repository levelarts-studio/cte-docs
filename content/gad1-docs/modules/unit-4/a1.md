## 🎥 Video Outline

- **Sculpting vs Modeling**
  - Modeling = Architecture (Verts/Edges).
  - Sculpting = Clay (Push/Pull).
- **The Brushes**
  - **Draw Sharp**: Good for creases.
  - **Clay Strips**: Good for building muscle.
  - **Grab**: Good for moving big shapes.
  - **Smooth (Shift)**: The eraser.

## 🛠️ Workshop Goals

- Take a simple Sphere.
- Use **Dyntopo** (Dynamic Topology) to add geometry as you paint.
- Sculpt a **Monster Head**.
- Don't worry about topology or edge loops yet. Just have fun.

## 📑 Deliverables

- **Screenshot**: Your sculpted monster head.
- Upload to **Canvas**.
