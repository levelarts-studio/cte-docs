## 🎥 Video Outline

- **The Problem**
  - High Poly models are too heavy for games (millions of tris).
  - Low Poly models look blocky.
- **The Solution**
  - We draw a new, clean low-poly mesh _over_ the high poly one.
- **Snap to Face**
  - Setting up Blender to stick vertices to the surface.

## 🛠️ Workshop Goals

- If your Unit 3 mesh is already clean, you might skip this step!
- If you sculpted new major forms in A4.2, you must **Retopologize**.
- Create a low-poly shell that wraps around your high-poly sculpt perfectly.
- Target: Under 10,000 Tris.

## 📑 Deliverables

- **Wireframe Screenshot**: Showing your clean low-poly mesh over the high-poly form.
- Upload to **Canvas**.
