## 🎥 Video Outline

- **The 5-Second Rule**
  - Recruiters spend 5 seconds per reel. Put your BEST work first.
- **Music**
  - No Dubstep. No Copyrighted music. Keep it generic and driving.
- **Pacing**
  - Cut to the beat.
  - Don't linger on a static image for more than 4 seconds.
- **Contact Info**
  - Start and End with your Name and Email.
