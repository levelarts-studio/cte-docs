  Portfolio Development (Year 2 Capstone)
</div>

By the end of Year 2, students should have a "Junior Environment Artist" or "Junior Character Artist" portfolio. This unit focuses on taking all the assets created this year and presenting them professionally.

{{< callout type="info" >}}
A folder of files is not a portfolio. A portfolio is a curated presentation of your **best work**.
{{< /callout >}}

## Topics Covered

- **The Audit**: Deciding what to keep and what to kill.
- **ArtStation**: Setting up a professional profile.
- **The Showreel**: Editing a 60-second video of your work.
- **Resume**: Updating skills with specific software (Blender 4.0, Unreal 5).
- **Online Presence**: How to get hired (or accepted to college).

## Learning Report

**Objectives**

- Critically analyze own work and select the best pieces.
- Create a professional ArtStation account.
- Edit a Video Demo Reel with music and titles.
- Write a Resume that highlights specific technical skills.

**Materials**

- Premiere Pro / DaVinci Resolve.
- All assets from Year 2.

**Standards Alignment**

- **4.6** — Maintain a portfolio of original works
- **3.2** — Develop a career plan and identify job opportunities
- **2.4** — Demonstrate ability to communicate using professional terminology
