This unit is the **capstone for Game Art & Design I**. Building on the environment you blocked out and tested in Unit 5, you will now continue developing the space into a completed, polished environment. This includes refining prop placement, enhancing landscapes and foliage, adding lighting for atmosphere, and implementing simple interactive elements with Blueprints. The project concludes with portfolio-ready renders and a walkthrough presentation.

{{< callout type="important" >}}
This unit is about **iteration and polish**. You already have a basic environment from Unit 5. Now you must refine it into a cohesive, professional-looking project for your portfolio.
{{< /callout >}}

## Topics Covered
- Refining blockouts into designed spaces  
- Prop polish and environment storytelling through placement  
- Advanced landscape sculpting and material blending  
- Lighting and post-processing for mood and clarity  
- Adding simple Blueprint interactions (e.g., lights, doors, particles)  
- Capturing high-quality renders and walkthrough videos

## Learning Report

**Objectives**  
- Develop a basic blockout into a polished environment scene  
- Refine prop placement and use visual storytelling techniques  
- Enhance landscapes, foliage, and lighting for mood and believability  
- Add at least one interactive element using Blueprints  
- Capture renders and a walkthrough presentation for portfolio inclusion  

**Materials**  
- Classroom computer with Unreal Engine installed  
- The environment started in Unit 5  
- Props created in Blender (Units 1–3)  

**Assessment**  
Students are evaluated on their ability to take an existing WIP environment and refine it into a complete, presentable scene. Grading focuses on technical workflow, integration of assets, mood and atmosphere, and clarity of presentation.  

**Activities**  
Students will create an iteration plan for their Unit 5 environment, then progressively refine it through prop polish, lighting passes, and interactive elements. Peer reviews will help identify areas for improvement. The final deliverable includes high-quality renders, a short video walkthrough, and a presentation board.
