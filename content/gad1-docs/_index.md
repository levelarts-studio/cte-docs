## Overview

Year 2 focuses on **technical mastery of Blender**, specifically training you to model and texture the conceptual assets you designed in Year 1. Instruction is modular—learn one tool, apply it immediately to your capstone asset. By year's end, you'll have a complete 3D scene ready for integration into Unreal Engine in Year 3.

## Primary Learning Objective

By the end of Year 2, students will have created **production-ready 3D assets** for their capstone project, including:
- A complete textured prop from concept
- A full character base mesh with UV mapping and textures
- An environment scene blocked out and ready for final rendering
- Understanding of professional 3D workflows and export pipelines

## Core Software & Tools

- **Blender** - 3D modeling, UV mapping, texturing, and basic rigging
- **Krita** - Creating custom textures for 3D models
- **File Export Pipelines** - Preparing assets for game engines

## Prerequisites — What You Should Know

Before starting Year 2, you should have completed Year 1 and have:

- **Complete Concept Document** — Your world, narrative, and character backstories defined
- **Character & Prop Concept Art** — 2D sketches and designs ready to model
- **Mood Boards & Style Guide** — Visual direction for your 3D work
- **Scene Planning** — Storyboards and shot lists for your final environment
- **Basic Krita Proficiency** — You'll continue using Krita for texture creation
- **Organized Asset Management** — File structure and naming conventions in place

If any of these areas are incomplete, revisit **Year 1 modules** before moving forward. Year 2 builds directly on Year 1's foundation.

## What We Cover

### Units 1-2: Blender Intro & Prop Modeling
**Focus:** Mastering Blender navigation and basic modeling tools to create your prop asset

**You will learn:**
- Blender interface and navigation (viewport, tools, shortcuts)
- Core modeling tools: extrude, loop cut, inset, bevel
- Manipulating vertices, edges, and faces
- Clean geometry principles and edge flow (AVG.17.5)
- Modeling your designed prop from concept art
- Export pipelines for 3D assets

**Standards:** AVG.17.5 Skill Building; 4.3 Technology (job-specific software); AVG.18.6 Process and Practice

### Units 3-4: Character Modeling & UVs
**Focus:** Modeling character base meshes and learning UV mapping/texturing workflows

**You will learn:**
- Character modeling from concept (base mesh construction)
- Topology considerations for characters
- Proportions and form accuracy
- UV mapping fundamentals
- Unwrapping strategies for characters and props
- Creating custom textures in Krita (AVG.17.8)
- Applying textures to 3D models
- Texture baking basics

**Standards:** AVG.17.5, AVG.17.8 Skill Building; AVG.18.2 Process and Practice (understanding artist workflows)

### Units 5-6: Environment Blocking & Practice
**Focus:** Blocking out environment scenes and preparing assets for final rendering

**You will learn:**
- Environment layout and scene composition
- Modular asset creation for environments
- Lighting fundamentals in Blender
- Basic rigging principles (AVG.17.6) for character posing
- Simple armatures and weight painting
- Posing your character for the final scene
- Integrating peer critique for iterative design (5.3, 7.4)
- Professional scene assembly and organization

**Standards:** AVG.17.6 Skill Building; 5.3 Critical Thinking; 7.4 Leadership; 9.1 Collaboration

## Semester Projects

### Project 2 (End of Unit 6)
**Full Scene with Character, Props, and Environment**

## Teaching Philosophy: Tool-First, Apply-Immediately

Each week follows a consistent pattern:
1. **Learn a specific Blender tool or technique**
2. **Apply it immediately to your capstone asset**
3. **Receive feedback and iterate**

This approach ensures you're not just following tutorials—you're building real production skills while creating your own unique project.

## Looking Ahead to Year 3

Everything you model in Year 2 will be imported into Unreal Engine in Year 3. You'll add professional lighting, visual effects, and final polish. The better your models and textures are in Year 2, the more impressive your final renders will be in Year 3.

## Assessment & Skill Development

Students are assessed on:
- **Skill Building (AVG.17.5, 17.6, 17.8):** Modeling, rigging, and texturing proficiency
- **Process & Practice (AVG.18.2, 18.6):** Professional workflows and industry-standard techniques
- **Technology (4.3):** Mastery of job-specific software (Blender, Krita)
- **Critical Thinking (5.3):** Problem-solving in 3D production challenges
- **Collaboration (9.1):** Peer critique, feedback integration, and teamwork
- **Technical Quality:** Clean geometry, proper UV mapping, efficient topology
