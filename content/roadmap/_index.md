The Game Art & Design Pathway is a **three-year program (18 units total)** that progressively builds technical and creative skills. Students develop a single capstone project from initial concept through final production, mastering industry-standard tools and professional workflows along the way.

## Program Philosophy

This curriculum follows a **tool-first, apply-immediately** approach. Each year focuses on specific software and skill sets that build upon previous learning:

- **Year 1:** Establish narrative and visual foundations using accessible 2D tools
- **Year 2:** Master technical 3D modeling and production workflows in Blender
- **Year 3:** Integrate assets into Unreal Engine and prepare professional portfolio materials

Students work on a **continuous capstone project** across all three years, with milestone projects every semester (6 total).

## Standards Alignment

This pathway aligns with the **California Arts, Media, and Entertainment (AME) Career Technical Education Industry Skills Framework (2024)**:

- **Anchor Skills (1.0–12.0):**
  Communication, technology use, critical thinking, collaboration, creative process, and career planning.

- **Cross-Cutting Skills (13.0–16.0):**
  Business/marketing, legal responsibility, media content creation, and production/project management.

- **Pathway Skills (17.0–19.0):**
  From the **Design, Visual, and Media Arts Pathway**, **Animation, Visual Effects, and Games (AVG)** focus area:
  - **Skill building (AVG.17.0):** Art and design principles, modeling, rigging, texturing, lighting, visual effects, rendering.
  - **Process & practice (AVG.18.0):** Production pipelines, industry-standard software, teamwork, iterative workflows.
  - **Career exploration (AVG.19.0):** Roles such as concept artist, 3D modeler, technical artist, environment artist, game designer.

## Three-Year Capstone Journey

Throughout the pathway, students develop a **single cohesive project**:
- **Year 1:** Concept, narrative, and visual development
- **Year 2:** 3D asset creation (character, prop, environment)
- **Year 3:** Final integration, rendering, and portfolio presentation

This continuous project approach mirrors professional game production pipelines and provides students with a comprehensive portfolio piece demonstrating mastery across all phases of development.
