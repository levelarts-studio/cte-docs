You will design **one original character** for a game world. This project pulls together the skills you’ve built in the first three units:  
- Understanding the **roles in the game industry** (Unit 1)  
- Practicing **digital literacy and professional file management** (Unit 2)  
- Applying **visual storytelling and game design fundamentals** (Unit 3)  

Your character concept should communicate personality, purpose, and world-fit. Think about how this character’s **role** would contribute to gameplay or narrative.  

> [!NOTE]  
> In **Semester 2**, you’ll expand this project by designing a **prop** for your character and building it as a 3D model in Blender. Plan ahead now — what tools, weapons, or objects does your character need?

---

# Deliverables

1. **Character Bio (150–200 words)**  
   - Role in the game world  
   - Habitat or setting  
   - Personality and temperament (reactive, calm, mischievous, etc.)  

2. **Concept Art**  
   - At least 2–3 silhouette explorations  
   - Refined sketches with readable proportions  
   - Final character sheet with front + profile views (3/4 optional)  

3. **Expression Sheet**  
   - 4–6 facial expressions showing personality range  

4. **Color & Materials**  
   - Palette exploration  
   - Final flats with material/texture notes  

5. **Presentation Board**  
   - A clean, single board combining art, bio, and notes  
   - Proper labels and captions for clarity  

6. **Process PDF**  
   - Iteration stages (silhouettes → sketches → finals)  
   - Reference images **with credit**  
   - Reflection on what you changed and why  

7. **Professional Practices**  
   - Correct file naming (`Last_First_Character_Final_v01.ext`)  
   - Organized folder structure  
   - Sources credited on boards and in PDF

> [!WARNING]  
> This is **not a drawing contest**. I don’t expect polished professional artwork. I expect **effort, iteration, and clear design thinking**.  If you show me you tried, you’ll get full credit. **Use references** — they’re essential, not optional. The more you push yourself, the more you’ll grow.

## Timeline & Milestones

| Milestone | Module | Deliverables |
|-----------|--------|--------------|
| **Concept Pack** | Week 2 | Bio, mood board, silhouettes |
| **Sketch Refinement** | Week 3 | Proportion studies, construction lines |
| **Expression + Orthographic** | Week 4 | Expressions + front/side views |
| **Final Submission** | Week 5 | Presentation board + Process PDF |

## Learning Outcomes

By completing this project, you will:  
- Practice the **role of a character artist** in the game pipeline  
- Show **professional file management and digital workflow**  
- Apply **visual storytelling** to design personality and function  
- Build a starter piece for your **portfolio**  

> [!TIP]  
> Focus on **design over polish**. A rough but well-planned sheet with references and notes is worth more than a “pretty picture” with no thought behind it.  

> [!WARNING]  
> Submissions **without references** will lose points. Always credit the artists and sources you study.

## Rubric

| Criteria | Exceeds (100%) | Meets (85%) | Approaches (55%) | Does Not Meet (0%) | Value |
|---|---|---|---|---|---:|
| **Research & Bio** | Comprehensive, annotated board; bio integrates role and world | Complete board; clear bio with context | Partial/vague; weak context | None submitted | 10 |
| **Silhouette Exploration** | 20–30 varied; strong shape language; 3 refined read clearly | 15+ with variety; 3 refined | Limited variety; weak refinement | \<5 submitted | 15 |
| **Sketches & Proportions** | Clean construction; consistent proportions; solid callouts | Mostly consistent; basic callouts | Inconsistent; missing callouts | None | 10 |
| **Orthographic Turnaround** | 4 views aligned; landmarks consistent | 3–4 views; minor misalignments | Incomplete or inconsistent | None | 20 |
| **Expression Sheet** | 6–8; clear range and personality | 4–5; some range | Minimal variation | None | 10 |
| **Color & Materials** | Strong palette study; clean flats; clear material notes | Palette explored; flats present | Minimal color work | None | 10 |
| **Final Boards** | Polished layout; labeled; portfolio-ready | Complete; legible | Rushed; inconsistent labels | None | 15 |
| **Process PDF & Reflection** | All stages; full sources; meaningful reflection | Major stages; sources; brief reflection | Minimal sources/reflection | None | 5 |
| **Professional Practices** | Correct naming; versions; credits on boards | Minor issues | Frequent issues | Disorganized; no credits | 5 |

*Late work: −10% per week. Missing sources: −5. Poor naming: −2.*
