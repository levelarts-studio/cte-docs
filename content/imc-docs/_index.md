Year 1 establishes the narrative and visual foundation for your three-year capstone project. You'll master accessible 2D tools (Krita, Canva, Google Docs) while building foundational design skills that prepare you for 3D production in Year 2.

## Primary Learning Objective

By the end of Year 1, students will have created a comprehensive **Concept Document** and **Visual Development Package** for their capstone project, including:
- World-building and narrative foundation
- Character and prop concept art
- Mood boards and visual style guide
- Scene planning with storyboards and shot lists
- Professional asset management and file organization

## Core Software & Tools

- **Krita** - 2D digital painting and concept art
- **Canva** - Visual presentations, mood boards, and storyboarding
- **Google Docs** - Concept documentation and collaborative writing
- **Google Drive** - Asset management and file organization

## What We Cover

### Units 1-2: Concept & Narrative
**Focus:** Defining the world (environment, history, audience) and creating a comprehensive Concept Document

**You will learn:**
- World-building fundamentals: setting, history, cultures
- Character backstories and motivations
- Target audience identification
- Professional concept documentation in Google Docs
- Narrative structure and visual storytelling principles

**Standards:** 1.3, 1.4 Academics; 4.2 Technology; 12.1 Audience and Behavior

### Units 3-4: Visual Development
**Focus:** Creating character and prop concepts using Krita and developing mood boards/visual pitches using Canva

**You will learn:**
- Line quality, form, and shape language
- Color theory and palette development
- Character design fundamentals (silhouettes, expression, anatomy basics)
- Prop and asset concept sketching
- Creating professional mood boards and style guides
- Visual pitch presentation techniques

**Standards:** 10.2, 10.3 Creative Process; 17.2 Skill Building (anatomy/expression—2D sketch level)

### Units 5-6: Media Planning & Project Setup
**Focus:** Planning the final scene with storyboards and establishing professional asset management workflows

**You will learn:**
- Shot composition and camera angles
- Storyboarding techniques in Canva
- Creating shot lists for scene planning
- Professional file naming conventions
- Asset tracking and organization in Google Drive
- Pre-production planning for 3D development

**Standards:** 15.4 Media Content Creation; 16.2 Production/Project Management; 4.4, 4.6 Technology

## Semester Projects

### Project 1 (End of Unit 3)
**Complete Concept Document with Initial Visual Development**
- Comprehensive world-building document
- Character and prop concept sketches
- Initial mood boards

### Project 2 (End of Unit 6)
**Full Pre-Production Package**
- Finalized Concept Document
- Complete visual style guide and mood boards
- Scene storyboards and shot lists
- Organized asset management system ready for Year 2 production

## Looking Ahead to Year 2

Everything you create in Year 1 will become the blueprint for Year 2's 3D modeling work. Your concept art, character designs, and prop sketches will be modeled in Blender. Your scene plans will guide environment construction. Strong foundational work in Year 1 makes Year 2 production much more efficient and focused.

## Assessment & Skill Development

Students are assessed on:
- **Creative Process (10.0):** Iterative development, research, and refinement
- **Technical Fluency (4.0):** Effective use of Krita, Canva, and Google Docs
- **Communication (1.0, 2.0):** Clear documentation and visual presentation
- **Academic Skills (1.4):** Research, writing, and critical thinking in concept development
- **Project Management (16.2):** Organization, planning, and asset tracking
