## 🏁 Final Unit Challenge

You've learned the tools, mastered the layers, and designed a character. Now, export your final avatar and add it to your portfolio.

## ✅ Checklist

- [ ] **Cleanup**: Hide sketch layers.
- [ ] **Crop**: Use the Crop tool to frame your portrait nicely.
- [ ] **Export**: File > Export > PNG.
- [ ] **Portfolio**:
  - [ ] Create a "Unit 4" (or "Art") page.
  - [ ] Upload the PNG.
  - [ ] Write a clear Title ("My RPG Avatar").
  - [ ] Add a Caption: "Created in Krita using a wacom tablet."

## 🚀 Submission Guidelines

1. **Export** your final image as a PNG.
2. **Post** it to your Portfolio Site.
3. **Submit** the link to your Portfolio Page in Canvas.

## 💯 Grading Rubric

- [ ] **Technical (40%)**: Clean lines, no stray pixels, layers used correctly (if checking source file).
- [ ] **Creativity (40%)**: Clear personality in the design.
- [ ] **Portfolio (20%)**: Correctly uploaded and captioned.
