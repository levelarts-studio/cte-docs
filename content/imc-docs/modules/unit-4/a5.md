<div class="hx:mt-1 hx:text-gray-500 hx:text-lg">
  Assignment 4.5 — Visual Pitch Presentation
</div>

This week, you'll present your complete visual development package to the class, practicing professional presentation skills.

## Assignment Directions

### Part 1: Refine Your Style Guide

Based on peer feedback from A4.4:
- Make final adjustments to your style guide
- Ensure all pages are polished and professional
- Check for consistency and clarity
- Add any missing elements

### Part 2: Create Presentation Slides

In Canva, create a 5-7 slide presentation deck covering:
1. **Title slide**: Project name, your name
2. **Concept overview**: World, narrative, character (1-2 slides)
3. **Visual development**: Mood board, color palettes (1-2 slides)
4. **Character & prop showcase**: Final colored designs (1-2 slides)
5. **Next steps**: Brief mention of Year 2 plans (modeling these designs)

Keep slides clean, visual, and easy to follow.

### Part 3: Present to Class

Deliver a 3-5 minute presentation covering:
- Your capstone concept (world + character + narrative)
- Your visual development process
- Final character and prop designs with color
- How your visual choices support the narrative

Practice beforehand to stay within time limit.

### Part 4: Semester 1 Reflection

Write a comprehensive reflection (300-400 words) on your first semester:
- What did you learn about concept development?
- How did your project evolve from Unit 1 to Unit 4?
- What are you most proud of?
- What will you focus on in Units 5-6?
- How do you feel about modeling these designs in Year 2?

## What to Turn In

1. Final refined style guide (Canva link or PDF)
2. Presentation slides (Canva link or PDF)
3. Presentation delivery (in-class or recorded video)
4. Semester 1 reflection (300-400 words)

## Grading Criteria

- **Refined Style Guide** (25%): Professional quality, complete
- **Presentation Slides** (20%): Clear, well-organized, visual
- **Presentation Delivery** (30%): Clear communication, time management, confidence
- **Reflection** (25%): Thoughtful analysis of learning and growth

## Congratulations!

You've completed **Units 1-4** and have:
- A complete Concept Document (world, narrative, characters)
- Character and prop concept art
- Professional color palettes
- A comprehensive visual style guide

In **Units 5-6**, you'll add scene planning, storyboarding, and asset management—completing your pre-production package for Year 2!
