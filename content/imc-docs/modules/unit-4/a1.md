## 🛠️ Workshop Goals

- Open Krita and create a new **A4 300 DPI** canvas.
- Test 3 different brushes.
- Practice navigating the canvas:
  - **Spacebar + Drag**: Pan.
  - **Ctrl + Space + Drag**: Zoom.
  - **Shift + Space + Drag**: Rotate Canvas.

## 📑 Deliverables

- **Screenshot** of your Krita workspace with scribbles showing you used 3 brushes.
- Upload to **Canvas**.
