  Assignment 4.2 — Developing Color Palettes
</div>

This week, you'll create specific color palettes for your character, props, and environment using the color theory principles from A4.1.

## Assignment Directions

### Part 1: Character Color Palette

Develop 3 different color palette options for your character (5-7 colors each):
- Include primary costume/body colors
- Accent colors for details
- Consider character personality and role
- Show how colors work together

Present each palette as color swatches with hex codes or RGB values.

### Part 2: Environment Color Palette

Create 2 color palette options for your environment/scene (6-8 colors each):
- Sky/atmosphere colors
- Landscape/surface colors
- Accent colors for points of interest
- Consider time of day and mood

### Part 3: Color Application Tests

In Krita, apply ONE character palette and ONE environment palette to rough sketches:
- Quick color tests (don't worry about polish)
- See how colors interact
- Test readability and contrast
- Make adjustments as needed

### Part 4: Final Selection & Justification

Choose your final palettes and write 200-250 words explaining:
- Why these colors fit your narrative and mood
- How they support your character's personality
- What color harmony you're using and why
- How character and environment colors work together

## What to Turn In

1. 3 character palette options (swatches with values)
2. 2 environment palette options (swatches with values)
3. Color application tests in Krita (images or file)
4. Final selection justification (200-250 words)

## Grading Criteria

- **Palettes** (40%): Well-considered, harmonious color choices
- **Application Tests** (30%): Clear demonstration of how colors work
- **Justification** (30%): Thoughtful reasoning connecting color to concept

**Standards Addressed**: 17.2 (Color theory application), 10.3 (Generating ideas), 10.5 (Iteration)
