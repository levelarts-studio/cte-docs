## 🎥 Video Outline

- **Shape Language**
  - **Circle**: Soft, Friendly, Safe (Kirby, Mario).
  - **Square**: Strong, Stable, Immovable (Ralph, Hulk).
  - **Triangle**: Sharp, Dangerous, Fast (Sonic, Villains).
- **The Silhouette Test**
  - If you black out the character, can you still recognize them?
  - Can you tell their personality just from the shadow?

## 🛠️ Workshop Goals

- Create a canvas.
- Using a large hard brush (Black color only):
  - Draw 5 Character Silhouettes.
  - 1 must be "Friendly" (Rounded).
  - 1 must be "Dangerous" (Spiky).
  - 1 must be "Strong" (Blocky).
- Do NOT draw details (eyes, clothes) inside. Just the outline.

## 📑 Deliverables

- **PNG Export** of your 5 silhouettes.
- Upload to **Canvas**.
