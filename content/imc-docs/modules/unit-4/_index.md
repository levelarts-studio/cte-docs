  Digital Illustration (Krita)
</div>

In this unit, students transition from vectors to raster graphics, learning the fundamentals of digital painting and illustration using **Krita**. This unit emphasizes non-destructive workflows (layers) and artistic principles like shape language and composition.

{{< callout type="info" >}}
This is the second creative unit, focusing on **illustration** and **character design** basics.
{{< /callout >}}

## Topics Covered

- **Raster vs Vector**: Understanding pixels.
- **Krita Interface**: Brushes, Erasers, and Navigation.
- **Layers**: Background, Mid-ground, Foreground.
- **Shape Language**: How shapes convey personality (Circle=Cute, Triangle=Dangerous).
- **Character Design**: Creating a digital avatar.

## Learning Report

**Objectives**

- Navigate the Krita interface and use basic brush tools.
- Demonstrate understanding of layer management.
- Apply shape language to character design.
- Create a polished digital avatar for the portfolio.

**Materials**

- Krita (Free Software).
- Drawing Tablet (optional but recommended).

**Assessment**  
Students demonstrate mastery by producing a low-poly model of their original prop, with clean topology, UVs, and a basic textured render uploaded to their portfolio.

**Activities**  
Students practice navigation and tool use through short guided exercises. They block out their prop in simple geometry, refine it with clean topology, unwrap UVs, and apply textures. They then render a turntable or screenshot for their portfolio.
