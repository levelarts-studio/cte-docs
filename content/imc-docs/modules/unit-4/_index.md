  Visual Development II: Color, Mood Boards & Visual Pitches in Canva
</div>

In Unit 4, you'll expand your visual development work by adding **color theory** and creating professional **mood boards and visual style guides** using Canva. You'll also develop a visual pitch that communicates your project's complete aesthetic.

{{< callout type="info" >}}
This unit transforms your black-and-white concepts into full-color visual communication. You'll learn how professionals present ideas to teams and clients.
{{< /callout >}}

## Topics Covered
- Color theory fundamentals: hue, saturation, value, temperature
- Color psychology and emotional impact
- Creating color palettes for characters and environments
- Introduction to Canva for presentation design
- Mood board creation and visual reference curation
- Visual pitch/style guide development
- Presenting design concepts professionally

## Learning Report

**Objectives**
- Understand and apply color theory principles to design
- Create harmonious color palettes that support narrative and mood
- Use Canva effectively for professional presentation design
- Curate and organize visual references into coherent mood boards
- Develop a comprehensive visual style guide for the capstone project
- Present design concepts in a clear, professional visual pitch

**Materials**
- Computer with internet access (Canva is web-based and free)
- Character and prop concepts from Unit 3
- Reference images collected throughout Units 1-3
- Krita for adding color to character concepts
- Concept Document from Units 1-2

**Assessment**
Students demonstrate visual development skills through color palette creation, mood boards, and a comprehensive visual style guide. Assessment focuses on color theory application, visual cohesion, and professional presentation quality.

**Activities**
Students study color theory through analysis of existing work, develop multiple color palette options, learn Canva through guided tutorials, curate mood boards showing their project's visual direction, add color to their character and prop concepts, and create a polished visual style guide/pitch deck.

## Connection to Your Journey

The visual style guide you create in Unit 4 will:
- Guide all color decisions when texturing 3D models in Year 2
- Inform lighting and atmosphere choices in Unreal Engine (Year 3)
- Serve as the visual "bible" for maintaining consistency across your entire capstone project
- Demonstrate your ability to develop and communicate a cohesive visual language

## Semester 1 Milestone

Unit 4 marks the end of your first semester (Units 1-3 complete conceptual and narrative foundation; Unit 4 completes visual development). You'll prepare your **Semester 1 Project** showcasing:
- Complete Concept Document (Units 1-2)
- Character and prop concepts (Unit 3)
- Color palettes and visual style guide (Unit 4)

This becomes your first major portfolio piece!
