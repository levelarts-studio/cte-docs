## 🎥 Video Outline

- **Elevator Pitches**
  - Can you explain your idea in 30 seconds?
- **The "Hook"**
  - What makes it unique?
  - "It's [Game A] meets [Game B] but with [Twist]."
- **Genre & Tone**
  - Is it a "Dark Fantasy RPG" or a "Cozy Farming Sim"?

## 🛠️ Workshop Goals

- Brainstorm 3 original ideas for a game or short film.
- Choose your favorite one.
- Write a **1-Paragraph Synopsis** (The Hook).
  - Must include: **Genre**, **Protagonist**, **Goal**, and **Conflict**.
  - Example: "A pizza delivery driver in a cyberpunk city must race against time to deliver a bomb-strapped package before it explodes, all while being hunted by rival gangs."
