  Media Planning & Project Setup I: Scene Planning & Storyboarding

In Unit 5, you'll plan the specific scene from your narrative that you'll create in 3D. You'll develop shot lists, storyboards, and composition plans using Canva, preparing for Year 2's modeling phase.

{{< callout type="info" >}}
This unit bridges visual development and 3D production. By planning your scene thoroughly now, you'll know exactly what to model in Year 2.
{{< /callout >}}

## Topics Covered
- Scene planning and shot composition
- Storyboarding techniques and conventions
- Camera angles and framing
- Shot lists and scene breakdowns
- Composition principles (rule of thirds, leading lines, depth)
- Creating storyboards in Canva
- Pre-visualization for 3D scenes

## Learning Report

**Objectives**
- Understand storyboarding conventions and visual language
- Apply composition principles to shot planning
- Create effective shot lists that break down scenes
- Develop clear storyboards using Canva
- Plan a 3D scene with specific camera angles and framing
- Prepare complete pre-visualization for modeling phase

**Materials**
- Canva (web-based)
- Concept Document and visual style guide from Units 1-4
- Reference examples of storyboards from films/games
- Notebook for planning and sketching

**Assessment**
Students demonstrate scene planning skills through shot lists and storyboards created in Canva. Assessment focuses on clarity of visual communication, effective composition, and practical planning for 3D production.

**Activities**
Students analyze existing storyboards, study composition principles through shot analysis, create shot lists breaking down their chosen scene, develop thumbnail storyboards, and create polished storyboard panels in Canva showing their complete scene plan.

## Connection to Your Journey

The scene plan and story boards you create in Unit 5 will:
- Define exactly what you need to model in Year 2
- Guide camera placement and framing in Unreal Engine (Year 3)
- Ensure your 3D work serves the narrative you developed
- Help you manage scope—model only what's needed for your scene
