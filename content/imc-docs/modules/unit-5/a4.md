## 🎥 Video Outline

- **Visual Grammar**
  - **Wide Shot (WS)**: Shows context/setting.
  - **Medium Shot (MS)**: Shows dialog/action.
  - **Close Up (CU)**: Shows emotion/detail.
- **Camera Angles**
  - **High Angle**: Makes subject look small/weak.
  - **Low Angle**: Makes subject look big/powerful.
  - **Dutch Angle**: Creates unease.
