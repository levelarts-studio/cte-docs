## 🏁 Final Unit Challenge

You have the story, the vibe, and the shot list. Now, draw it. You will create a standard film/game storyboard to visualize your scene.

## ✅ Checklist

- [ ] **Method**: Use Krita (Digital) or Pencil/Paper (Traditional).
- [ ] **Panels**: Draw the 6 shots from your A5.4 Shot List.
- [ ] **Clarity**: Stick figures are okay! Focus on composition and camera angle.
- [ ] **Arrows**: Use arrows to show movement (camera or character).
- [ ] **Text**: Add a small caption under each panel describing the action.

## 🚀 Submission Guidelines

1. **Export/Scan** your storyboard as a PDF or JPG.
2. **Post** it to your Portfolio Site under "Unit 5".
3. **Submit** the link to your Portfolio Page in Canvas.

## 💯 Grading Rubric

- [ ] **Continuity (40%)**: Does the sequence make sense?
- [ ] **Cinematography (40%)**: Did you use different shot types (WS, CU)?
- [ ] **Completeness (20%)**: All 6 panels drawn and captioned.
