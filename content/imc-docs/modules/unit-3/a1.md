## 🛠️ Workshop Goals

- Find 3 **Job Postings** online for distinct entry-level roles (e.g., "Junior Enviro Artist", "QA Tester", "Social Media Intern").
- **Analysis**: For each job, list:
  1. The Job Title & Company.
  2. One "Hard Skill" required (software/technique).
  3. One "Soft Skill" required (communication/teamwork).

## 📑 Deliverables

- **Job Analysis Doc**: A simple Google Doc with links to the 3 jobs and your analysis notes.
- Upload to **Canvas**.
