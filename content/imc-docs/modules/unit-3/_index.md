  Career Exploration (Midterm)
</div>

This unit shifts focus to the real world. Students investigate careers in the game and media industries, identifying roles that match their skills and interests. They will learn to speak the language of professional job applications by creating a resume, cover letter, and a career research presentation.

{{< callout type="info" >}}
This is the **midterm project** for Semester 1. It focuses on **future planning** and **professionalism**.
{{< /callout >}}

## Topics Covered

- **Industry Roles**: Understanding the difference between entry-level vs senior roles.
- **Job Hunting**: Reading and analyzing job descriptions.
- **Professional Documents**: Writing Resumes and Cover Letters.
- **Presentation Skills**: Creating a slide deck to teach others (the Feynman Technique).

## Learning Report

**Objectives**

- Identify 3 distinct entry-level roles in the game/media industry.
- Analyze job descriptions to understand required skills.
- Create professional application documents (Resume, Cover Letter).
- Present research findings in a visual slide deck.

**Materials**

- Google Docs (Resume/Cover Letter).
- Google Slides (Deck).
- Job Boards (LinkedIn, ArtStation, Indeed - view only).

**Assessment**  
Students show mastery by creating a concept art sheet that demonstrates drawing fundamentals and communicates a clear design idea. Portfolio entries should include both process work and the final design.

**Activities**  
Students complete daily drawing warm-ups, create thumbnail variations of their chosen prop or environment, and refine one design into a polished piece. They share their process in class critiques, then finalize and publish their work to their portfolio.
