  Assignment 3.4 — Prop Concept Sketches
</div>

Props tell stories. A worn sword, a glowing artifact, or a simple tool can reveal character history and world details. This week, you'll design props that support your narrative and character.

## What Makes a Good Prop Design?

- **Functionality**: Does it look like it would work? (Even in fantasy settings)
- **Story Connection**: Does it relate to the character or world?
- **Visual Interest**: Is it more interesting than a basic real-world object?
- **Appropriate Complexity**: Matches the world's technology/culture level

## Assignment Directions

### Part 1: Prop Research

Find and analyze **3 prop designs** from games or films (weapons, tools, artifacts, everyday objects). For each:
1. Include a reference image
2. Write 2-3 sentences: What makes this prop design effective?
3. How does the prop reflect the world/character it belongs to?

### Part 2: Prop Brainstorming

Based on your character and narrative, identify **3 props** your character would carry, use, or interact with. For each prop:
- What is it? (Name and basic function)
- Why does your character have it? (Story connection)
- What should it look like? (Materials, style, wear and tear)

Write 2-3 sentences for each prop explaining its significance.

### Part 3: Prop Thumbnail Sketches

In Krita, create **6-8 thumbnail sketches** for ONE of your three props, exploring different design directions:
- Different shapes and proportions
- Varying levels of detail or decoration
- Different states (new vs. worn, folded vs. extended, etc.)
- Alternative materials or construction

Keep thumbnails small and quick (5 minutes each).

### Part 4: Refined Prop Sketch

Choose your best thumbnail and create a **refined concept sketch**:
- Larger size (quarter to half of your canvas)
- Multiple views if helpful (front, side, detail)
- Add basic shading to show form
- Include notes or annotations (materials, scale, special features)

This doesn't need to be a finished illustration—it should clearly communicate the design.

## What to Turn In

Submit to Canvas:
1. Prop research analysis (3 examples with images and explanations)
2. Brainstorming notes for 3 props (2-3 sentences each)
3. 6-8 prop thumbnails in Krita
4. 1 refined prop concept sketch with annotations

## Grading Criteria

- **Research** (20%): Thoughtful analysis of existing prop designs
- **Brainstorming** (20%): Props connected to story and character
- **Thumbnails** (30%): Variety of explorations, clear iteration process
- **Refined Sketch** (30%): Clear communication of final design

## Looking Ahead

The prop you design this week will:
- Be included in your visual development package
- Potentially be modeled in 3D in Year 2
- Appear in your final Unreal scene in Year 3

Choose something meaningful but achievable to model!
