## 🎥 Video Outline

- **What is a Cover Letter?**
  - It's a "Love Letter" to the company.
  - "I am applying for X role because Y."
- **Structure**
  - **The Hook**: Grab attention.
  - **The Meat**: Connect your skills to their needs.
  - **The Ask**: Ask for an interview.
- **Tone**
  - Professional but enthusiastic.
