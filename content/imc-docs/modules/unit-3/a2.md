  Assignment 3.2 — Shape Language & Silhouettes
</div>

Shape language is how character designers communicate personality through basic shapes. This week, you'll explore how circles, squares, and triangles create different feelings and learn to design readable silhouettes.

## Understanding Shape Language

- **Circles/Curves**: Friendly, approachable, soft, harmless
- **Squares/Rectangles**: Stable, reliable, strong, grounded
- **Triangles**: Dynamic, dangerous, energetic, sharp

Professional character designs combine these shapes intentionally to communicate personality at a glance.

## Assignment Directions

### Part 1: Shape Language Analysis

Find 3 characters from games, films, or animation. For each, create a simple analysis in Krita or on paper:
1. Screenshot or reference image of the character
2. Draw the character's silhouette as a solid black shape
3. Overlay basic geometric shapes (circles, squares, triangles) showing the shape language
4. Write 2-3 sentences: What personality does the shape language communicate?

### Part 2: Shape Language Experiments

In Krita, design 3 simple character silhouettes (just black shapes, no details) using different shape languages:
1. **Circle-based character**: Friendly, approachable
2. **Square-based character**: Strong, stable
3. **Triangle-based character**: Dynamic, aggressive

Each silhouette should be **immediately readable**—you should be able to tell what it is even as a black shape.

### Part 3: Apply to Your Character

Look at your character profile from Unit 2. Based on their personality:
- What shape language fits them best?
- Why does this shape language match their personality?
- Sketch 2-3 quick silhouette ideas for your character using appropriate shapes

Write a short justification (100-150 words) explaining your shape language choices.

## What to Turn In

Submit to Canvas:
1. Shape language analysis of 3 existing characters (images with annotations)
2. 3 experimental silhouettes in Krita (circle, square, triangle-based)
3. 2-3 silhouette sketches for your capstone character
4. Shape language justification (100-150 words)

## Grading Criteria

- **Analysis** (30%): Clear identification of shape language in existing characters
- **Experiments** (35%): Three distinct, readable silhouettes
- **Character Application** (20%): Appropriate shape choices for personality
- **Justification** (15%): Clear reasoning connecting shape to character

## Pro Tip

Silhouettes are the **first test** of character design. If your design doesn't read well as a black shape, it won't read well in full color either. Start simple!
