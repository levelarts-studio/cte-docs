<div class="hx:mt-1 hx:text-gray-500 hx:text-lg">
  Assignment 3.3 — Character Design Thumbnails
</div>

Professional concept artists create dozens of **thumbnail sketches** before committing to a final design. Thumbnails let you explore many ideas quickly without investing too much time in any single one.

## What Are Thumbnails?

Thumbnails are small (literally thumbnail-sized), rough sketches that explore different design directions. They're about **exploration, not perfection**.

**Goal**: Quantity over quality. Generate lots of ideas, then choose the best to refine.

## Assignment Directions

### Part 1: Thumbnail Studies (Practice)

Before designing your character, warm up with thumbnail studies of existing characters:
- Find 3 characters you admire
- For each, create 3-5 quick thumbnail sketches (5 minutes each maximum)
- Focus on capturing the **essence** not the details

This teaches you to see the "big shapes" that make designs work.

### Part 2: Character Thumbnail Exploration

Create **12-15 thumbnail sketches** exploring different designs for your main character. Each should be:
- Small (2-3 inches on your canvas)
- Quick (5-10 minutes each)
- Exploring different directions (varied silhouettes, proportions, details)

**Guidelines**:
- Use your character's personality and shape language as a guide
- Try different proportions (tall/short, thin/bulky)
- Experiment with clothing, accessories, posture
- Keep it loose—these are sketches, not finished art

### Part 3: Thumbnail Selection

From your 12-15 thumbnails:
1. **Choose your top 3 favorites**
2. For each, write 2-3 sentences explaining why it works
3. **Select 1 final design** you'll refine in future assignments
4. Explain why this design best represents your character (100-150 words)

## What to Turn In

Submit to Canvas:
1. Thumbnail studies of existing characters (3 characters, 3-5 sketches each)
2. 12-15 original character thumbnails (Krita file or images)
3. Top 3 selections with explanations
4. Final design selection with justification (100-150 words)

## Grading Criteria

- **Thumbnail Studies** (20%): Practice thumbnails show observational skill
- **Character Thumbnails** (40%): 12-15 varied, exploratory sketches
- **Selection Process** (25%): Clear criteria for choosing top designs
- **Final Justification** (15%): Thoughtful reasoning for final choice

## Pro Tip

If you're struggling to generate 12-15 different ideas, try:
- Changing proportions (head-to-body ratio)
- Adding/removing clothing layers
- Changing pose or stance
- Varying level of detail or ornamentation

Constraints breed creativity!

{{< callout type="warning" >}}
Don't fall in love with your first idea! Professional designers explore extensively before committing. Your 10th thumbnail might be better than your 1st.
{{< /callout >}}
