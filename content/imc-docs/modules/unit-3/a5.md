## 🏁 Final Unit Challenge

You have researched your future, written your professional documents, and built a presentation. Now, you will curate this into a dedicated "Career" page on your portfolio site.

## ✅ Checklist

- [ ] **Create Page**: New page titled "Career Research" on your Google Site.
- [ ] **Embed Resume**: Embed the PDF of your resume.
- [ ] **Embed Deck**: Embed your Google Slide presentation.
- [ ] **Reflection**: Add a text box with:
  - "The role I researched is..."
  - "I am interested in this because..."
- [ ] **Publish**: Don't forget to hit the blue button!

## 🚀 Submission Guidelines

1. **Open your Portfolio Site**.
2. Navigate to the **Career Research** page.
3. **Copy the link**.
4. **Submit the URL** to Canvas.

## 💯 Grading Rubric

- [ ] **Completeness (40%)**: Resume and Deck are embedded and visible.
- [ ] **Presentation (40%)**: Layout is clean, text is readable.
- [ ] **Content (20%)**: Resume and Deck meet requirements from previous assignments.
