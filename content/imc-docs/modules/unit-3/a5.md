  Assignment 3.5 — Refined Character Concept
</div>

This week, you'll take your chosen character thumbnail from A3.3 and refine it into a polished character concept sheet. This will serve as the primary reference for all future work on this character.

## What is a Character Concept Sheet?

A character concept sheet typically includes:
- Multiple views (front, side, back) OR
- One detailed view with expression/pose variations
- Clear line work showing design details
- Optional: notes on materials, colors (to be added in Unit 4), scale

## Assignment Directions

### Part 1: Line Art Refinement

Using your selected thumbnail as reference, create a refined line drawing in Krita:
1. Start with a rough sketch layer to work out proportions
2. Create a clean line art layer on top
3. Focus on clarity—every line should communicate information
4. Show details: clothing folds, accessories, facial features, etc.

**Choose ONE of these approaches**:
- **Option A**: Three views (front, 3/4, side or back)
- **Option B**: One detailed view + 3-4 facial expressions or poses

### Part 2: Basic Value/Shading

Add simple shading to your line art:
- Create a new layer below your line art
- Use 2-3 values (light, mid-tone, shadow)
- Show form and volume
- Keep it simple—full rendering comes later

Purpose: Make sure the design reads well in 3D space.

### Part 3: Annotations

Add text annotations to your concept sheet:
- Height reference (compare to average human, or give actual height)
- Material notes (fabric types, metal, leather, etc.)
- Special features or details that need explanation
- Personality reminders ("confident stance," "worn edges show age," etc.)

### Part 4: Reflection

Write a short reflection (150-200 words):
- How did your character design evolve from thumbnail to final?
- What design choices best communicate their personality?
- What was challenging about translating concept to visual?
- How will this design guide your 3D modeling in Year 2?

## What to Turn In

Submit to Canvas:
1. Refined character concept sheet (Krita file + exported PNG/JPG)
2. Concept sheet with annotations and notes
3. Reflection (150-200 words)

## Grading Criteria

- **Line Art Quality** (35%): Clear, confident lines; well-considered details
- **Value/Shading** (25%): Effective use of shading to show form
- **Annotations** (20%): Helpful notes that clarify design choices
- **Reflection** (20%): Thoughtful analysis of design process
