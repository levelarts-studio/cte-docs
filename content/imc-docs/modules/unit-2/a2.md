  Assignment 2.2 — Cultures & Societies
</div>

Every world has cultures and societies that shape how people live, think, and interact. This week, you'll develop the cultural elements of your world.

## Assignment Directions

### Part 1: Cultural Development

Write a detailed description (300-400 words) of one primary culture or society in your world. Include:
- **Social structure**: How is society organized? (Classes, tribes, guilds, etc.)
- **Values and beliefs**: What matters most to this culture?
- **Customs and traditions**: Daily rituals, celebrations, or ceremonies
- **Relationship to the environment**: How do they interact with your world's geography?

### Part 2: Cultural Artifacts

Describe 3-5 cultural artifacts or practices that define this society:
- Art styles or architectural elements
- Clothing or personal adornment
- Tools, weapons, or technology
- Music, storytelling, or communication methods

### Part 3: Cultural Conflicts

Write a short analysis (150-200 words): How does this culture create potential for conflict or tension? Consider internal divisions or external pressures.

## What to Turn In

Submit to Canvas:
1. Cultural description (300-400 words)
2. List of 3-5 cultural artifacts with descriptions
3. Cultural conflicts analysis (150-200 words)

## Grading Criteria

- **Cultural Description** (45%): Rich detail, internally consistent
- **Cultural Artifacts** (30%): Creative and appropriate to the culture
- **Conflicts** (25%): Shows critical thinking about tension sources

**Standards Addressed**: 1.4 (Academic skills - social studies), 10.2 (Research), 10.3 (Generating ideas), 1.3 (Research)
