## 🎥 Video Outline

- **Type Terminology**
  - **Serif**: Has "feet" (Times New Roman). Traditional, serious.
  - **Sans Serif**: No feet (Arial, Helvetica). Modern, clean.
  - **Display / Decorative**: Fun, but hard to read.
- **Hierarchy**
  - **The Title**: Big, bold, grabs attention.
  - **The Header**: Organizes sections.
  - **Body Copy**: Small, readable, for the details.
- **Combining Fonts**
  - The "One Serif, One Sans" rule.
