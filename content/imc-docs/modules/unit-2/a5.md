## 🏁 Final Unit Challenge

You have established your colors, your fonts, and your logo. Now, combine them into a single professional document: Your **Digital Style Guide**. This guide defines "Who You Are" as an artist.

## ✅ Checklist

- [ ] **Cover Page**: With your Name and Loop.
- [ ] **Page 1: The Logo**: Show your logo in Color and Black & White.
- [ ] **Page 2: Typography**: Show your Header and Body font choices (A-Z, 0-9).
- [ ] **Page 3: Color Palette**: Your 5 chosen brand colors with Hex codes.
- [ ] **Portfolio Update**:
  - [ ] Create a "Unit 2" page on your Google Site.
  - [ ] Embed your Style Guide PDF.
  - [ ] Add a short reflection on why you chose these colors/fonts.

## 🚀 Submission Guidelines

1. **Export** your Style Guide as a **Standard PDF**.
2. **Post** it to your Portfolio Site.
3. **Submit** the link to your Portfolio Page in Canvas.

## 💯 Grading Rubric

- [ ] **Completeness (40%)**: Logo, Fonts, and Colors all present.
- [ ] **Design (40%)**: The guide itself looks good (not messy).
- [ ] **Portfolio (20%)**: Correctly uploaded and linked.
