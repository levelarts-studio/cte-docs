  Assignment 2.5 — Finalizing the Concept Document (Units 1-2)
</div>

This week marks the completion of the **Concept & Narrative** phase of Year 1. You'll compile all your character and narrative work into your Concept Document and prepare for the visual development phase.

## Assignment Directions

### Part 1: Update Your Concept Document

Add the following sections to your Google Doc with all work from Unit 2:

**New Sections to Add:**
- **Character Profiles**: Main character and supporting characters (from A2.1)
- **Cultural Development**: Society descriptions and artifacts (from A2.2)
- **Central Conflict**: Conflict statement and development (from A2.3)
- **Narrative Arc**: Full story outline and key scenes (from A2.4)
- **Selected Scene Description**: The specific scene you'll depict

### Part 2: Create a Visual Story Map

Using Google Slides, Canva, or hand-drawing, create a visual story map showing:
- Your narrative arc as a flow or timeline
- Key characters and their relationships
- The central conflict visualized
- Your chosen scene highlighted

### Part 3: Unit 2 Reflection

Add a new reflection section (200-250 words) addressing:
- How have your characters and narrative evolved since you started?
- What surprised you during the character/story development process?
- How do the characters fit into the world you created in Unit 1?
- What are you excited to visualize in the next phase?

### Part 4: Prepare for Visual Development

Create a "Visual Development Prep" section listing:
- What your main character might look like (preliminary thoughts)
- What props or objects are important to your story
- What the setting/environment of your chosen scene needs
- Any visual references you've already been thinking about

## What to Turn In

Submit to Canvas:
1. Updated Concept Document link (with all Unit 2 content)
2. Visual story map (uploaded as image/PDF or link)

## Grading Criteria

- **Document Completeness** (40%): All Unit 2 content properly integrated
- **Visual Story Map** (25%): Clear, organized, visually effective
- **Reflection** (20%): Thoughtful analysis of development process
- **Visual Prep** (15%): Shows forward thinking toward next phase

## Looking Ahead

**Congratulations!** You've completed the Concept & Narrative phase. In Units 3-4, you'll begin **Visual Development**, where you'll:
- Design your characters using Krita (2D concept art)
- Create props and asset concepts
- Develop mood boards and style guides in Canva
- Establish the visual language of your world

Everything you've written will now be translated into visual form!
