  Assignment 2.1 — Creating Character Backstories
</div>

Characters are the heart of any story. This week, you'll develop detailed backstories for the main characters in your capstone project, giving them depth, motivation, and personality.

## Assignment Directions

### Part 1: Primary Character Profile

Create a detailed profile for your main character (300-400 words) including:
- **Name and basic description**
- **Background**: Where they're from, family, upbringing
- **Personality traits**: 3-5 defining characteristics
- **Goals and desires**: What do they want?
- **Fears and weaknesses**: What holds them back?
- **Unique details**: Quirks, habits, or defining features

### Part 2: Supporting Characters

Create profiles for 2-3 supporting characters (150-200 words each). Include:
- Name and role in the story
- Relationship to the main character
- Basic personality and motivation
- How they contribute to the narrative

### Part 3: Character Motivations

Write a short analysis (200-250 words) exploring:
- What drives your main character's actions?
- How do their past experiences shape who they are?
- What internal or external factors create tension?

## What to Turn In

Submit to Canvas:
1. Primary character profile (300-400 words)
2. 2-3 supporting character profiles (150-200 words each)
3. Character motivations analysis (200-250 words)

## Grading Criteria

- **Primary Character** (40%): Detailed, well-developed, compelling
- **Supporting Characters** (30%): Clear roles and personalities
- **Motivations Analysis** (30%): Shows understanding of character psychology

**Standards Addressed**: 1.4 (Academic skills), 2.7 (Writing), 10.2 (Research), 10.3 (Generating ideas)
