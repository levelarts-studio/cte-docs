## 🛠️ Workshop Goals

- Open Canva and create a blank "Presentation (16:9)" canvas.
- Use only **black** squares, circles, and triangles.
- Create 3 pages:
  1. A **Symmetrical** composition (Order).
  2. An **Asymmetrical** composition (Chaos).
  3. A creature made entirely of primitive shapes.

## 📑 Deliverables

- **Export**: Download your 3 pages as a single PDF.
- Upload to **Canvas** as **"Composition Study"**.
