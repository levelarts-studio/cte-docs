## 🎥 Video Outline

- **What makes a good logo?**
  - **Simple**: Can you draw it from memory?
  - **Scalable**: Does it look good on a billboard AND a business card?
  - **Relevant**: Does it fit your vibe?
- **Vector vs Raster**
  - Introduction to "shapes" in Canva vs "pixels" in paint programs.
- **Monograms vs Icons**
  - Using your initials (Monogram) vs a symbol (Icon).

## 📚 Resources

## 🛠️ Workshop Goals

- Sketch 5 ideas on paper first.
- Move to Canva:
  - Create a **Monogram** version (using your initials).
  - Create a **Symbol** version (using shapes/icons).
- Refine your favorite one using your **Color Palette** from Assignment 2.2.

## 📑 Deliverables

- **Logo Sheet**: Containing your rough sketches (photo) and your final digital logo.
- Upload to **Canvas**.
