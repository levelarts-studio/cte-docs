## 🎥 Video Outline

- **Who is this for?**
  - "Everyone" is a wrong answer.
- **Demographics**
  - Age, Gender, Location.
- **Psychographics**
  - Interests: "Likes Anime," "Plays D&D," "Watches Twitch."
- **Player Types**
  - **Achievers**: Want points/ranks.
  - **Explorers**: Want story/lore.
  - **Socializers**: Want to play with friends.
  - **Killers**: Want to win/dominate.
