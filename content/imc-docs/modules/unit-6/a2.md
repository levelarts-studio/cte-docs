## 🎥 Video Outline

- **What is Key Art?**
  - The Single Image that sells the game (Box Art, Netflix Thumbnail).
  - Must establish **Genre**, **Tone**, and **Action**.
- **Composition Tricks**
  - **Center Focus**: Hero in the middle.
  - **Vs**: Hero vs Villain.
  - **Ensemble**: The whole team.

## 📚 Resources

## 🛠️ Workshop Goals

- Open Krita.
- Create a **Poster Format** canvas (11x17 or similar vertical ratio).
- **Composite**:
  - Bring in your Avatar (A4.5).
  - Bring in Background elements from your Mood Board (A5.3) or paint simple ones.
  - Add the **Title** of your game at the top or bottom (Use your Fonts!).
- **Goal**: Make it look like a real movie poster or game box.

## 📑 Deliverables

- **Key Art JPG**: High-quality export.
- Place this image into **Slide 6** of your Pitch Deck.
- Upload to **Canvas**.
