## 🏁 Final Year 1 Challenge

You have done it. You have navigated year 1, learned the tools, and built a concept. Now, package it all up for your final exam grade.

## ✅ Checklist

- [ ] **Portfolio Page**: Create a new page called "Final Project".
- [ ] **Banner**: Use your Key Art (A6.2) as the header.
- [ ] **Introduction**: Your "Hook" text.
- [ ] **Embeds**:
  - [ ] The Pitch Deck (Google Slides).
  - [ ] The Pitch Video (YouTube/Drive/Loom embed).
  - [ ] Gallery of your process (Avatar, Mood Board, Storyboard).
- [ ] **Reflection**: "What I learned in Year 1."

## 🚀 Submission Guidelines

1. **Publish** your Portfolio.
2. **Double Check** all links and embeds.
3. **Submit** the URL to Canvas.

## 💯 Grading Rubric

- [ ] **Completeness (40%)**: Video, Deck, and Art present.
- [ ] **Quality (40%)**: Effort shown in Key Art and Pitch.
- [ ] **Reflection (20%)**: Thoughtful writing about the year.
