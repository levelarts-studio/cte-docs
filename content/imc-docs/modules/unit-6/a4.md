## 🎥 Video Outline

- **Public Speaking Tips**
  - Stand up (even if recording audio).
  - Smile (it changes your voice).
  - Slow down!
- **The Script**
  - Don't read the slides.
  - Tell a story.
  - "Imagine a world where..."

## 🛠️ Workshop Goals

- **Write a Script**: 60 seconds max.
- **Record**:
  - Option A: Record yourself presenting the slides (Picture-in-Picture).
  - Option B: Record just audio over the slides.
- **Review**: Watch it back. Can you hear yourself? Do you sound confident?
