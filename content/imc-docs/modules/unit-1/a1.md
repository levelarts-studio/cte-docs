Welcome to the **Arts, Media, and Entertainment (AME) pathway**.  

So, what does that mean? Let’s break it down:

- **CTE** stands for **Career Technical Education**. It’s school, but with a career focus. Instead of just learning math, science, or history in isolation, CTE courses connect your learning directly to skills and jobs. Think of it as “school that points you toward the real world.”  

- **AME** is one of California’s 15 official CTE sectors. Each sector is like a big umbrella of related industries. AME covers careers in:  
  - **Design, Visual, and Media Arts**  
  - **Performing Arts**  
  - **Production and Managerial Arts**  
  - **Game Design and Integration** (that’s us!)  

Sectors are broken into **pathways** — a focused track of study. You’re in the **Game Art & Design Pathway**, which means your classes will center on skills like 3D modeling, visual storytelling, environment design, and building interactive projects in game engines like Unreal.

**Why are we covering this?**  
Because understanding the big picture matters. You’re not just “learning Blender” or “doing a school project.” You’re building **portfolio pieces** that line up with real career roles in game studios, animation, and interactive media. Every project you do here is designed to prepare you for that future.

### Quick Questions to Think About
- When you think “game industry,” what jobs come to mind first? Artist? Coder? Producer?  
- Which sounds most exciting to you right now — **making things look amazing** (art), **making things work** (programming), or **making sure the whole team finishes a game** (production)?  
- Do you imagine yourself working solo, freelancing, or as part of a big studio team?
