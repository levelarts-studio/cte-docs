## What is World-Building?

World-building is the process of creating a fictional setting with its own rules, history, geography, and culture. It's the foundation for:
- Video games (The Legend of Zelda, Dark Souls, Horizon)
- Animated films (How to Train Your Dragon, Spider-Verse)
- Books and comics (Lord of the Rings, Avatar: The Last Airbender)

Great worlds feel **consistent, believable, and immersive**—even if they include magic, aliens, or impossible physics.

### Key World-Building Elements

1. **Setting**: Where does your world exist? (Fantasy realm, sci-fi planet, historical period, alternate reality)
2. **Tone**: What's the feeling? (Dark and mysterious, bright and whimsical, gritty and realistic)
3. **Rules**: What's possible? (Magic systems, technology level, natural laws)
4. **Purpose**: What story do you want to tell in this world?

## Assignment Directions

### Part 1: Explore Existing Worlds (Research)

Choose **two different fictional worlds** from games, movies, or books. For each, write a short analysis (100-150 words):

- What makes this world memorable?
- How does the visual style reflect the world's tone?
- What specific details help you understand this world quickly?

**Examples to consider**:
- Games: Hollow Knight, Journey, Breath of the Wild, Ori and the Blind Forest
- Films: Studio Ghibli movies, Into the Spider-Verse, Wall-E
- Books/Comics: The Hobbit, Amulet, Bone

### Part 2: Brainstorm Your World (Ideation)

Answer these questions in your notebook or document:

1. **Genre**: What genre interests you most? (Fantasy, sci-fi, post-apocalyptic, historical fantasy, cyberpunk, etc.)
2. **Inspiration**: What games, movies, or art styles inspire you?
3. **Core Idea**: In one sentence, what is your world about?
4. **Feeling**: What emotion should someone feel when they see your world?

### Part 3: Initial Concept Statement

Write a short paragraph (150-200 words) describing the world you want to create for your capstone project. Include:
- Basic setting (where/when)
- Tone and atmosphere
- One unique feature that makes it interesting

**Example**: *"My world is a floating archipelago of islands held aloft by ancient crystals. It's a high-fantasy setting with a hopeful, adventurous tone inspired by Studio Ghibli and Breath of the Wild. The unique feature is that the islands are slowly falling, and inhabitants must work together to keep them airborne using the power of music and crystal resonance."*

## What to Turn In

Submit to Canvas:

2. Answers to the brainstorming questions
