This week, you'll compile everything you've developed into the **beginning of your Concept Document** in Google Docs. This living document will grow throughout Year 1 and serve as the blueprint for your entire three-year capstone project.

## What is a Concept Document?

A Concept Document (sometimes called a "Design Bible" or "Art Bible") is a comprehensive guide that contains:
- World-building details
- Visual references
- Character and prop concepts
- Narrative elements
- Technical specifications

Professional studios use concept documents to ensure everyone on a team understands the creative vision.

## Assignment Directions

### Part 1: Set Up Your Google Doc

Create a new Google Doc titled: **"[Your Name] - Capstone Concept Document"**

Set up the following sections with headers:

1. **Project Overview**
2. **World Environment**
3. **World History**
4. **Target Audience**
5. **Visual References**
6. **Character Concepts** (placeholder for now)
7. **Prop Concepts** (placeholder for now)
8. **Scene Planning** (placeholder for now)

### Part 2: Compile Your Work

Take the content you've created over the past four weeks and organize it into your Concept Document:

- **Project Overview**: Your initial concept statement from A1.1
- **World Environment**: Environment description, map image from A1.2
- **World History**: Timeline and historical deep dive from A1.3
- **Target Audience**: Audience analysis from A1.4
- **Visual References**: Embed or link your mood board from A1.2

### Part 3: Polish and Format

Make your document professional and easy to read:
- Use consistent heading styles
- Include images where appropriate
- Add a table of contents (Insert > Table of contents in Google Docs)
- Check spelling and grammar
- Use clear, organized formatting

### Part 4: Reflection (NEW Content)

Add a final section called **"Unit 1 Reflection"** and write 150-200 words reflecting on:
- What have you learned about world-building?
- What are you most excited about for your project?
- What challenges do you anticipate?
- How has your concept evolved since Week 1?

## Grading Criteria

- **Organization** (25%): Clear structure, proper headers, table of contents
- **Content Compilation** (40%): All previous work included and properly formatted
- **Visual Presentation** (15%): Professional appearance, images embedded well
- **Reflection** (20%): Thoughtful reflection showing growth and planning

In Unit 2, you'll continue developing your Concept Document by adding character backstories, motivations, and narrative structure. Everything you've built so far provides the foundation for these character and story elements.
