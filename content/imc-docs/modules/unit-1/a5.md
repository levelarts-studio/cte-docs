## 🏁 Final Unit Challenge

This week, you are finalizing your digital foundation. You have your hardware set up, your files organized, and your portfolio shell built. Now it's time to polish it for a "Client Review" (your teacher).

## ✅ Checklist

- [ ] **Hardware**: Workstation is clean, cables are managed.
- [ ] **File Tree**: `Year_1` folder exists with `Unit_01` inside.
- [ ] **Digital Safety**: Quiz passed, passwords updated.
- [ ] **Portfolio**:
  - [ ] Home Page has a Title and Intro.
  - [ ] About Page has a bio.
  - [ ] Navigation menu works.
  - [ ] Site is **Published** (not just in Draft mode).

## 🚀 Submission Guidelines

1. **Open your Portfolio Site**.
2. Click the "Copy Published Site Link" icon.
3. **Test the link** in an Incognito window to make sure it works for others.
4. **Submit the URL** to the Unit 1 assignment slot in Canvas.

## 💯 Grading Rubric

- [ ] **Functionality (40%)**: Link works, navigation works, no broken images.
- [ ] **Content (40%)**: All required pages (Home, About) are present and filled out.
- [ ] **Professionalism (20%)**: Spelling, grammar, and clean formatting.
