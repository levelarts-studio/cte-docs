## 🎥 Video Outline

- **Why Organization Matters**
  - "Where did I save that?" = Wasting time.
  - The "Bus Factor": If you get hit by a bus, can your team find your files?
- **The Golden Rules of Naming**
  - No Spaces! Use underscores (`_`) or dashes (`-`).
  - Use version numbers (`_v01`, `_v02`).
  - Be descriptive: `Project_Asset_Version.ext`
- **The Directory Structure**
  - Create a "Master Folder" for the class.
  - Subfolders for each Unit.
  - Subfolders for "Assets", "References", "Exports".
