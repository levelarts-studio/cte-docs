  Careers in Games & Media Arts
</div>

This first unit introduces both the creative industries and the essential platforms you’ll use throughout the course. Students learn about careers in games and media arts, explore production pipelines, and get set up with the digital tools needed for all future work.

{{< callout type="info" >}}
This unit combines **career exploration** with **technology onboarding**. By the end, every student will have access to required accounts and understand how their personal interests connect to real opportunities in creative technology.
{{< /callout >}}

## Topics Covered
- Game industry roles: art, design, programming, production, audio, writing  
- Mapping careers to animation, VFX, and design pathways  
- Key production pipeline stages and terminology  
- Logging in and setting up required accounts (school email, Canvas, Jupiter)  
- Introduction to Canva for basic design, collaboration, and publishing

## Learning Report

**Objectives**  
- Recognize and describe roles in the game and media arts industries  
- Connect careers across animation, VFX, and design pathways  
- Identify and use production pipeline terminology  
- Set up and log into core class platforms (Canvas, Jupiter, Canva)  
- Create a first design project in Canva to practice publishing and collaboration  

**Materials**  
- Notebook or digital journal  
- Classroom computers with internet access  
- Accounts: Canvas, Jupiter, Canva  

**Assessment**  
Students demonstrate understanding of career pathways through written reflections and role research. Technical skills are demonstrated by successful login, account setup, and completion of a basic Canva design.  

**Activities**  
Students complete guided research, write career profiles, and map roles to AME pathways. In parallel, they set up required accounts, confirm access, and create a simple Canva project (slide deck, infographic, or poster) to practice core publishing and collaboration features.

- **3.1** — Explore creative and technical jobs across industries.  
- **3.2** — Conduct interest, aptitude, and career research to develop a plan.  
- **7.2** — Work collaboratively and follow through as a team member.  
- **11.1** — Demonstrate media literacy through critical inquiry and analysis.
