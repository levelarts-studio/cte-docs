  Final Project Rubric

You will work in **teams** to deliver a polished **vertical slice** of your game. This project connects directly to **Year 3, Semester 2** units:  
- **Unit 4 — Gameplay Systems & Interactive Elements**  
- **Unit 5 — Advanced Blueprints & Interactive Systems**  
- **Unit 6 — Final Vertical Slice Delivery**  

Your team’s final build should demonstrate **art, interactivity, and storytelling** at a professional standard. The vertical slice should feel like a complete, playable section of a game — polished enough to showcase to an outside audience.

> [!NOTE]  
> All members receive the **same grade** for the final project. This is a capstone team effort.

---

# Deliverables

Submit one complete **Vertical Slice Package** that includes:

1. **Packaged Build**  
   - Playable level with functioning gameplay systems.  
   - Custom character setup, navigation, and interaction.  
   - Optimized for stable performance.  

2. **Portfolio Assets**  
   - 4–6 beauty renders/screenshots of the environment.  
   - At least one turnaround or flythrough video.  
   - Captured trailer (30–60s) highlighting gameplay and mood.  

3. **Showcase Presentation**  
   - Team presentation of the vertical slice.  
   - Overview of process, key systems, and final results.

| **Showcase Delivery** | End of Unit 6 | Presentation, trailer, renders, and final vertical slice |

---

## Learning Outcomes

By completing this project, you will:  
- Deliver a playable **vertical slice** demonstrating advanced Unreal skills.  
- Implement **Blueprint logic and interactive systems** beyond the basics.  
- Apply **optimization, polish, and QA testing** for performance.  
- Produce **portfolio-ready renders, video, and documentation**.  
- Present work professionally in a showcase environment.  

---

## Submission Format

- Team folder labeled: `TeamName_VerticalSlice.zip`  
- Subfolders:  
  - `01_Build/` → final packaged Unreal project  
  - `02_Portfolio/` → screenshots, renders, video, trailer  
  - `03_Presentation/` → slides and showcase materials

## Rubric (100 pts)

| Criteria | Excellent | Adequate | Weak | Value |
|----------|-----------|----------|------|------:|
| **Gameplay & Interactivity** | Systems functional, immersive, and polished | Basic systems functional | Systems incomplete or broken | 25 |
| **Environment & Assets** | Cohesive, optimized, strong visual storytelling | Present but rough in places | Incomplete or inconsistent | 20 |
| **Lighting & Mood** | Professional quality, supports navigation | Functional but uneven | Poor or missing | 15 |
| **Portfolio Assets** | Polished renders, flythrough, and trailer | Some assets present | Minimal or missing | 15 |
| **Showcase Presentation** | Clear, professional, engaging | Present but unpolished | Weak or unclear | 15 |
| **Team Professionalism** | Deadlines met, files organized, project stable | Some issues | Disorganized or incomplete | 10 |

*Late work: −10% per week. Unstable or unplayable builds: −10 pts.*
