  Final Project Rubric
</div>

You will deliver a **Final Vertical Slice & Showcase Presentation** that represents the culmination of three years of learning. Working in teams, you will polish your Beta build into a professional, playable slice, create a full marketing package, and present your work at a public showcase.  

This project connects directly to **Year 3, Semester 2** units:  
- **Unit 5 — Vertical Slice Build (Beta + Marketing)**  
- **Unit 6 — Final Vertical Slice & Showcase**  

Your final deliverables must reflect both **technical execution** and **professional presentation**.

> [!IMPORTANT]  
> This project is worth **30% of your semester grade**. It requires consistent progress across the semester. Weekly work, milestones, and peer feedback all feed into the final showcase. 

# Steps to Completion

{{% steps %}}

### Step 1 — Final Build Polish  
Fix bugs, optimize performance, and finalize lighting, UI, and mechanics.  
Ensure the slice is stable, balanced, and playable from start to finish.  

### Step 2 — Playtesting & Feedback  
Conduct external playtests with classmates or outside players.  
Gather feedback, refine difficulty and pacing, and document changes.  

### Step 3 — Marketing Package  
Finalize your **game trailer** (30–90 sec), **cover art**, and **press kit**.  
The press kit should include: screenshots, description, team credits, and download links.  

### Step 4 — Showcase Presentation  
Prepare and rehearse a professional pitch presentation.  
This may be live or recorded but must clearly communicate your game, process, and team contributions.  

### Step 5 — Portfolio & Resume Finalization  
Update your personal portfolio with:  
- Screenshots and trailer of the final slice  
- Reflection on your team role and contributions  
- Final resume formatted for creative industry opportunities  

### Step 6 — Public Showcase Delivery  
Present your work to peers, faculty, and/or the community.  
Submit all packaged files, marketing materials, and portfolio entries.  

{{% /steps %}}

# Deliverables

- **Packaged Final Vertical Slice Build** — playable build + Unreal project folder  
- **Marketing Package** — trailer, cover art, press kit  
- **Showcase Presentation** — live or recorded pitch  
- **Portfolio Update** — final slice entry, reflection, trailer/screenshots  
- **Resume Update** — polished, professional resume  

## Timeline & Milestones

| Milestone | Week | Deliverables |
|-----------|------|--------------|
| **Polish & Debug Pass** | Weeks 1–2 | Final bug fixes, optimization |
| **Playtesting & Refinement** | Weeks 3–4 | Feedback report, balance changes |
| **Marketing Drafts** | Week 5 | Trailer rough cut, cover art sketch, press kit draft |
| **Showcase Prep** | Week 6 | Rehearsed pitch + packaged build |
| **Final Submission** | End of Unit 6 | Build, marketing, presentation, portfolio update |

## Learning Outcomes

By completing this project, you will:  
- Deliver a **polished, playable vertical slice** of a game  
- Apply **feedback and iteration** from external playtesting  
- Produce a professional **marketing package**  
- Present a game in a **showcase format** simulating industry pitch events  
- Finalize your **portfolio and resume** for career or college applications  


## Submission Format

- Folder labeled: `TeamName_FinalVerticalSlice.zip`  
- Subfolders:  
  - `01_Project/` → Unreal project folder  
  - `02_Build/` → packaged playable build  
  - `03_Marketing/` → trailer, cover art, press kit PDF  
  - `04_Presentation/` → slides or recorded showcase pitch  
  - `05_Portfolio/` → portfolio screenshots, resume, reflection  

## Rubric (100 pts)

| Criteria | Excellent | Adequate | Weak | Value |
|----------|-----------|----------|------|------:|
| **Final Build** | Polished, stable, fully playable | Playable, some rough edges | Unstable or incomplete | 25 |
| **Playtesting & Refinement** | Comprehensive report, feedback applied | Basic testing, partial changes | Minimal/no testing | 15 |
| **Marketing Package** | Professional-quality trailer, cover, and press kit | All materials present but basic | Missing or unprofessional | 20 |
| **Showcase Presentation** | Confident, clear, professional pitch | Clear but basic or uneven | Weak, incomplete, or missing | 20 |
| **Portfolio & Resume** | Fully updated, professional-ready | Updated, some issues | Incomplete or missing | 10 |
| **Professional Practices** | Organized files, naming, credits, deadlines met | Some issues | Disorganized or late | 10 |

*Late work: −10% per week. Missing credits: −5 pts. Poor naming/organization: −2 pts.*
