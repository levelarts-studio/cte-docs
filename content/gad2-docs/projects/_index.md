Final projects are the **capstone assignments for each semester**. They are long-term projects that you work on gradually while completing weekly assignments and unit work. These projects showcase your best skills at that point in the program and prepare you for professional portfolio standards.

Good final projects are:
- **Planned** — you start early and track progress each week.  
- **Iterative** — you revise based on critique and feedback.  
- **Documented** — you save sketches, drafts, and notes.  
- **Presented** — you explain your work clearly to an audience.  

# Semester 1 Final Project

<div class="hx:mt-1 hx:text-gray-500 hx:text-lg">
  Team Concept & First Playable
</div>

**Focus**: Pre-production and prototyping.  

**Task**: Work with your team to create a **Game Design Document (GDD)**, an **art style guide**, and a **first playable demo** of your project. This is your proof of concept — showing how your world, assets, and mechanics come together.  

Steps:  
- Form teams and assign roles (concept, modeling, design, tech).  
- Draft a GDD outlining vision, goals, and scope.  
- Create visual targets and style guides.  
- Assemble a playable blockout with major key props and basic navigation in Unreal.  

**Deliverables**:  
- Completed GDD and style guide  
- Visual target board(s)  
- First playable demo build (blockouts + key props, lighting, navigation)  
- Team presentation of concept and demo  

{{< callout type="info" >}}
This project connects to **Unit 3 — World Assembly & First Playable** and marks the first milestone for your capstone game.  
{{< /callout >}}

# Semester 2 Final Project

<div class="hx:mt-1 hx:text-gray-500 hx:text-lg">
  Final Vertical Slice Delivery
</div>

**Focus**: Production, polish, and professional presentation.  

**Task**: Deliver a polished **vertical slice** of your game project. This is the final capstone of the pathway — a playable level segment that demonstrates art, design, interactivity, and storytelling at a professional standard.  

Steps:  
- Implement advanced Blueprints and interactive systems.  
- Refine assets, textures, and lighting for performance and polish.  
- Package a final playable build.  
- Create marketing assets (screenshots, trailer, portfolio renders).  
- Present the final project at the showcase.  

**Deliverables**:  
- Packaged vertical slice build (Unreal project)  
- Portfolio renders/screenshots of environment and props  
- Short trailer or video walkthrough  
- Showcase presentation (team)  

{{< callout type="important" >}}
This project connects to **Unit 6 — Final Vertical Slice Delivery** and concludes the AME Game Art & Design pathway.  
{{< /callout >}}

## Portfolio Requirement

By the end of Semester 2, you will submit a **professional portfolio package** that includes:
- Semester 1 team concept package (GDD, style guide, first playable)  
- Semester 2 final vertical slice and marketing assets  
- Selected weekly assignments that demonstrate process and growth  
- An individual reflection on your skills, role, and future goals  

Your portfolio becomes the key showcase of your work for **college, internships, or industry opportunities**.  

---

## Grading & Structure

Final Projects count for **30–40% of the semester grade**.  
They are graded on:  
- **Creativity & Originality** — unique ideas, thoughtful design choices  
- **Technical Skill** — modeling, texturing, Blueprint scripting, level assembly  
- **Process** — research, iteration, revisions, feedback applied  
- **Presentation** — ability to explain work clearly to an audience  
- **Professionalism** — meeting deadlines, collaborating effectively, formatting portfolio work  

{{< callout type="warning" >}}
Final Projects are **make-or-break**. Perfect weekly points without a final project can only earn you a **C**. To earn an **A or B**, you must complete and present your Final Project.  
{{< /callout >}}
