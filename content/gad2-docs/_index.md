## Overview
Game Art & Design II is the capstone year of the pathway. Students work in teams to design, build, and deliver a polished vertical slice of a game level. This year emphasizes collaborative production, advanced Unreal workflows, optimization, and professional presentation.

{{< callout type="info" >}}
Looking for lessons and assignments? See the **[Modules Page](modules)**.
{{< /callout >}}

## Recap — What You Should Know
Before starting this course, you should already be confident with:

- **Blender Workflows** — modeling clean low-poly props, UV unwrapping, baking, and creating PBR textures.  
- **Unreal Engine Basics** — importing assets, setting up materials, organizing projects, and blocking out scenes.  
- **Environment Design** — assembling props into layouts, sculpting landscapes, adding foliage, and lighting for mood.  
- **Blueprint Fundamentals** — setting up simple interactions like doors, switches, or particle effects.  
- **Portfolio Practices** — documenting work with renders, process notes, and clear project files.  

If these skills feel weak, review your **Game Art & Design I environment project** before moving forward.

## What We Cover

{{% steps %}}

### Step 1 — Narrative Design & Game Design Document (Unit 1)  
Draft and refine a **Game Design Document (GDD)**.  
Outline narrative, mechanics, art style, and scope for your vertical slice.  

### Step 2 — Team Pitch & Pre-Production (Unit 2)  
Present your GDD in a **pitch deck**.  
Form teams, assign roles, and create production schedules and asset lists.  

### Step 3 — Prototyping & Learning Objectives (Unit 3)  
Build small prototypes to test mechanics, art style, or workflows.  
Define **team learning objectives** and set milestones for production.  

### Step 4 — Vertical Slice Alpha (Unit 4)  
Assemble an **Alpha build** in Unreal Engine.  
Integrate assets and core gameplay into a playable, start-to-finish slice.  

### Step 5 — Vertical Slice Beta + Marketing (Unit 5)  
Polish and refine systems into a **Beta build**.  
Add lighting, audio, effects, and create marketing materials: trailer, cover art, and press kit.  

### Step 6 — Final Vertical Slice & Showcase (Unit 6)  
Deliver a polished **Final Vertical Slice**.  
Present your game and marketing package in a public **showcase event** and finalize portfolios/resumes.
