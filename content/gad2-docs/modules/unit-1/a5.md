## 🎥 Video Outline

- **Purpose of the Finalized GDD**  
  - The GDD is your **blueprint** going into pre-production.  
  - Teams will use these documents in Unit 2 to decide which projects to pursue.  

- **What Should Be Included:**  
  - Title and high-level overview  
  - Narrative concept (Unit 1.1)  
  - Core gameplay loop (Unit 1.2)  
  - Features list (scope-appropriate)  
  - Art and style guide with references  
  - Technical notes (engine, platform, team tools)  
  - Production plan (milestones, roles)  

- **Tips for Finalizing:**  
  - Revise based on peer and instructor feedback (from A1.4).  
  - Ensure clarity — another team should be able to pick this up and build the slice.  
  - Keep formatting consistent and professional.  

- **Next Steps Reminder:**  
  - This document will be pitched in Unit 2.  
  - Strong clarity and scope now will make your pitch much easier.

## 🛠️ Workshop Goals  

**Workshop 1**  
- Review peer and instructor feedback from A1.4.  
- Revise sections that lacked clarity or scope.  
- Add visuals to the art/style guide (moodboards, palettes).  
- Ensure technical and production sections are complete.  

**Workshop 2**  
- Proofread and format the full GDD.  
- Export to PDF for submission.  
- Upload to Canvas and your portfolio.  
- Prepare 1–2 slides or summary points to support your pitch in Unit 2.  

*If you finish early:*  
- Create a short “pitch blurb” version of your GDD (2–3 sentences).  
- Add optional diagrams (level flow, UI mockups, mechanic flowcharts).  

## 📑 Deliverables

- **Finalized GDD** (PDF format) including:  
  - Overview, Narrative, Core Loop, Features List, Art/Style Guide, Technical Notes, Production Plan  
- **Portfolio Upload** with GDD file and short reflection  
- Upload to **Canvas** and **Portfolio** as:  
  **“Final Game Design Document”**
