## 🏁 Final Unit Challenge

Combine your composition, trim sheets, foliage, and shaders into one stunning image.

## ✅ Checklist

- [ ] **Composition**: Clear focal point using light.
- [ ] **Tech**: Trim sheets used for 50% of the scene.
- [ ] **Life**: Foliage or movement in the scene.
- [ ] **Specs**: Runs at 60fps (or is a high-quality cinematic render).

## 🚀 Submission Guidelines

1. **Render** a 4K Image from the Movie Render Queue.
2. **Post** to ArtStation.
3. **Submit** link to Canvas.

## 💯 Grading Rubric

- [ ] **Art (50%)**: Is it beautiful?
- [ ] **Tech (30%)**: Are the shaders and optimization smart?
- [ ] **Story (20%)**: Does the environment tell a story without words?
