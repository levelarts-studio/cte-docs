## 🎥 Video Outline

- **The Concept**
  - Instead of baking a unique normal map for every pillar...
  - Model a "Sheet" of textures (Trims, patterns, bolts).
- **UV Workflow**
  - Unwrapping the model to "Straighten" the UVS onto the Trim Sheet.
  - This is how Star Citizen and Sunset Overdrive were made.

## 🛠️ Workshop Goals

- Model a High Poly "Trim Sheet" (10-15 strips).
- Bake it to a single 2k texture (Normal/AO/Curvature).
- Create a texture from it.
- Model a Column, a Wall, and a Doorway using **ONLY** this one texture.

## 📑 Deliverables

- **Screenshot**: Your Trim Sheet texture + The assets mapped to it.
- Upload to **Canvas**.
