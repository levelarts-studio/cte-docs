  Career & College Launch
</div>

You made it. Two years of hard work. Now, we package it up and sell it. This unit is purely focused on getting you a job or into college.

{{< callout type="info" >}}
"Your portfolio is only as good as your worst piece." — Industry Adage. We will be cutting anything that isn't "Pro" level.
{{< /callout >}}

## Topics Covered

- **The Demo Reel**: Editing to music, cutting on the beat, showing your best work first.
- **ArtStation**: Tagging, categorization, and presentation.
- **The Application**: Resumes, Cover Letters, and LinkedIn.
- **Interview Prep**: Mock interviews and soft skills.

## Learning Report

**Objectives**

- Create a compelling Demo Reel (max 90 seconds).
- Build a professional "Junior" level ArtStation.
- Write a Resume that beats the ATS (Applicant Tracking System).
- Apply to a real job (Mock).

**Materials**

- DaVinci Resolve / Premiere.
- LinkedIn Account (Optional).

**Standards Alignment**

- **2.5** — Speak and present professionally when pitching work.
- **12.1** — Adapt career plans based on industry trends.
- **12.2** — Create a professional portfolio and resume.
