## 🎥 Video Outline

- **Rigify**
  - Stop building skeletons from scratch. Using Blender's built-in auto-rigger.
- **Face Rigging**
  - Adding bones for the jaw, eyes, and eyelids.
  - Shape Keys for expressions (Smile, Frown, Blink).
