## 🛠️ Workshop Goals

- Write a 1-page script (approx 1 minute).
- Draw a storyboard (12+ panels) visualizing the script.
- Focus on **visual storytelling** (Show, Don't Tell).

## 📑 Deliverables

- **PDF**: The Script.
- **Image**: The Storyboard.
- Upload both to **Canvas**.
