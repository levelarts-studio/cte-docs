## 🏁 Final Unit Challenge

Compile all your Pre-Production work into a single document that "Sells" your film.

## ✅ Checklist

- [ ] **Logline**: 1 Sentence summary.
- [ ] **Script**: Full text.
- [ ] **Art**: Character Designs and Environment Concepts.
- [ ] **Storyboards**: Key moments.
- [ ] **Previs**: Link to the video.

## 🚀 Submission Guidelines

1. **Create** a PDF "Pitch Bible".
2. **Post** to ArtStation (Blog or Project).
3. **Submit** link to Canvas.

## 💯 Grading Rubric

- [ ] **Cohesion (40%)**: Does the art match the story?
- [ ] **Clarity (30%)**: Can I understand the film just by reading this?
- [ ] **Planning (30%)**: Is the scope realistic?
