## 🎥 Video Outline

- **Scale Reference**
  - Always have a human-sized cube in the scene.
- **Blocking for Camera**
  - Don't build the whole world. Only build what the camera sees.
- **Parallax**
  - Putting objects in the foreground to create depth.

## 🛠️ Workshop Goals

- Block out the main set for your film.
- Use simple shapes (Cubes, Cylinders).
- Place cameras matching your storyboard shots.
- **Walk through it**: Does it feel the right size?
