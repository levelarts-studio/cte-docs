This site hosts all course content for the **Arts, Media & Entertainment (AME) Game Art & Design pathway developed by Level Arts Studio**. It provides lesson plans, assignments, resources, and standards alignment for students. The AME field evolves quickly with new tools, platforms, and industry practices. This site is regularly updated to reflect those changes so students are always building skills that connect directly to today’s creative industries.


---

## How to Use This Site
- **Modules**: Each course is organized into units. Each unit page lists assignments, topics, resources, and a learning report.  
- **Assignments**: Click an assignment card for full instructions, resources, and submission notes.  
- **Resources**: Downloadable guides, slides, and references are available on unit pages.  
- **Standards**: Each unit lists the California AME CTE standards it covers.  
- **Submissions & Grades**: If you are an Alder Grove student all work is turned in through **Canvas**, and grades are recorded in **Jupiter**. This site does not handle submissions or grades directly.  

{{< callout type="info" >}}
Think of this site as the **library and roadmap** of the program. Canvas is the **workspace** where you turn things in. Jupiter is the **gradebook** where you see results.
{{< /callout >}}

## Classroom Policies
- **Respect the space and the people**: professionalism, honesty, and collaboration are expected.  
- **Stay on task**: use computers and devices only for coursework.  
- **Deadlines**: weekly assignments are due in Canvas. Late work is accepted until the end of the unit with a point deduction.  
- **Integrity**: all work must be original. Cite sources and media when used.  
- **Safety**: no food or drink near computers; save and back up your work.  

---

## AI Use Policy
AI tools are increasingly part of creative industries. They are allowed here under specific guidelines:  

**Allowed**  
- Brainstorming ideas or outlines  
- Research support and technical help  
- Generating inspiration or references  
- Editing support (spelling/grammar checks)  

**Not Allowed**  
- Submitting AI-generated work as if it were your own  
- Using AI to bypass assignments or creative effort  
- Omitting credit when AI tools were used  

{{< callout type="warning" >}}
If AI contributes to an assignment, **you must cite it**. Example: *“Outline generated with ChatGPT, revised by student.”*
{{< /callout >}}
